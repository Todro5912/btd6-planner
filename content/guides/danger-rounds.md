# Danger Rounds

Have camo detection by R24, lead popping by R28 and a MOAB answer before R40 starts. After that the big checks are R63 (ceramic rush), R78 (camo ceramics), R90 and R95 (DDTs), R98 (highest RBE) and R100 (the BAD).

Cash figures are cumulative pop cash plus end-of-round bonus from R1, without the $650 start (calc). Hard starts at R3 and Impoppable / CHIMPS at R6, so subtract $258 or $735.

### DDT answers

A DDT is a fast MOAB-class blimp with Camo, Lead and Black properties. One tower needs camo detection **and** an attack that is neither sharp nor explosive (Normal, fire, plasma or acid). A camo Sharp tower can't hurt it, and nor can a Bomb.

| Approach | Options (Medium price) |
|---|---|
| One tower does it all | Sniper 1-1-0 ($950), Dart 4-0-2 ($2,950), Spike Factory 2-x-x ($2,400), Buccaneer 0-2-2 ($2,000), Wizard 0-1-2 ($1,150), Heli 3-2-0 ($5,550), Dartling 0-3-0 ($6,550), Super 2-0-2 ($11,200) |
| Give your sharp towers Lead | Acidic Mixture Dip (Alchemist 2-0-0, $1,150) next to a camo Sharp tower; [Gwendolin](/heroes/gwendolin/)'s Heat It Up (L4) |
| Give your Lead poppers camo | Radar Scanner (Village 0-2-0, $3,450) or [Etienne](/heroes/etienne/) L8, *only* if the Lead popper isn't explosive |
| Decamo | Signal Flare (Mortar 0-0-3, $2,300), Submerge and Support (Sub 3-0-0), Cleansing Foam (Engineer 0-3-0, $1,850) |
| Explosives on DDTs | [Striker Jones](/heroes/striker/) L19 removes Black immunity for explosives (50% chance from L5) |
| Everything at once | Monkey Intelligence Bureau (Village 0-3-0, $10,950): every tower in range can damage every type, and the path includes Radar Scanner, so they see camo too |
| Slow them | Bloon Sabotage (Ninja 0-4-0, $7,450) halves bloon speed |

Not DDT answers on their own: a Ninja with Flash Bomb (explosive), a Bomb Shooter, an Ice Monkey with Cold Snap (can't affect blimps), and any Sharp tower without lead help. Effects that only reach "up to BFB" skip DDTs, such as Abyssal Warrior's ink and Psi before level 20.

### The BAD on R100

- Single-target bursts: Spike Storm's ability, First Strike Capability timed after the BAD's last damage phase so it one-shots the insides, MOAB Eliminator, Legend of the Night.
- [Ezili](/heroes/ezili/)'s MOAB Hex deals percentage damage.
- Plan R98 → R99 → R100 as one sequence. Most players fire everything on R98 and have nothing for the Fortified DDTs on R99.

## Key rounds: cheap answers

Prices are Medium totals for the whole crosspath (tower plus upgrades). Multiply by about 1.08 on Hard and 1.2 on Impoppable/CHIMPS. Codes are top-middle-bottom paths.

**R24: 1 Camo Green.** The first camo; a tower without camo detection can't see or hit it, explosions included.
- Dart 0-0-2 Enhanced Eyesight: $490
- Ninja 0-0-0: $400 (camo built in)
- Sniper 0-1-0 Night Vision Goggles: $600

**R28: 6 Lead.** Sharp, cold and energy attacks (darts, tacks, shurikens, Ice, Wizard bolts) bounce off.
- Any Bomb Shooter: $375
- Sniper 1-0-0 Full Metal Jacket: $700
- Wizard 0-1-0 Fireball: $550, or Alchemist 2-0-0 ($1,150) next to your Dart Monkeys

**R33: 20 Camo Red, 13 Camo Yellow.** Too many for one 0-0-2 Dart.
- Dart 0-2-2 ($780) or 2-0-2 ($830)
- Ninja 2-0-1: $1,400
- Wizard 0-0-2 Monkey Sense: $850

**R37: 25 Black, 25 White, 15 Lead, 10 Zebra, 7 Camo White.** Bombs can't touch the Blacks or Zebras; darts can't touch the Leads.
- Sniper 1-1-0: $950 (pops all of it)
- Wizard 0-1-2: $1,150 (fire pops Lead, Black and White; Monkey Sense for the camo)
- Bomb 0-0-2 Frag Bombs ($875) beside a Dart 0-0-2 ($490)

**R40: 1 MOAB, 200 HP.** Any damage works; you just need enough of it focused on one target.
- Bomb 0-3-0 MOAB Mauler: $2,025
- Sniper 2-0-0 Large Caliber: $2,000 (2-1-0 for $2,250 adds camo)
- Alchemist 0-2-0 Perishing Potions: $1,275 (bonus MOAB damage; supports your other towers)

**R45: 10 Camo Purple, 4 Fortified Lead, 180 Pink.** Camo, Lead and no-magic all at once. Fortified Leads just have 4 HP.
- Ninja 2-0-1 ($1,400) plus any Bomb ($375)
- Spike Factory 2-0-0 White Hot Spikes: $2,400 (camo, Lead and Purple)
- Sniper 1-1-0: $950

**R50: 2 MOAB, 20 Ceramic, 8 Fortified Lead.**
- Bomb 2-3-0 MOAB Mauler + Heavy Bombs: $2,925
- Dart 4-0-2 Juggernaut: $2,950 (ceramics and Leads)
- Sniper 2-1-0: $2,250

**R63: 75 Lead and 122 Ceramic in 3 packed waves.** The top mid-game killer. You need area damage that pops Lead, spread over three waves.
- Bomb 2-0-4 Recursive Cluster: $4,975 (or 4-2-0 Bloon Impact, $5,825)
- Ice 3-2-0 ($2,900) plus Glue 2-3-0 ($3,425) to soften the waves
- Two Dart 4-0-2 Juggernauts: $2,950 each

**R76: 60 Regrow Ceramic, tightly packed.**
- Village 0-1-0 Grow Blocker: $1,450 in range of your damage
- Bomb 2-0-4: $4,975
- Dart 4-0-2: $2,950

**R78: 72 Camo Ceramic, 80 Purple, 150 Rainbow, 1 BFB.** About 90 s long. Bombs need camo help; Wizards can't touch the Purples.
- Village 0-2-0 Radar Scanner ($3,450) over a Bomb 2-0-4 ($4,975)
- Mortar 0-0-3 Signal Flare: $2,300 (gives the Mortar camo and decamos the wave for everyone)
- Ninja 4-0-2 Bloonjitsu: $5,500

**R90: 3 DDT behind 50 Fortified Camo Regrow Lead.** First DDTs. See the DDT answers below.
- Spike Factory 2-0-0 at the exit: $2,400
- Dartling 0-3-0 Hydra Rocket Pods: $6,550
- Sniper 1-1-0 ($950) for the Leads, plus MIB ($10,950) if your carry is a Bomb or a Sharp tower

**R95: 30 DDT, 50 Fortified MOAB, 500 Camo Regrow Purple, 250 Fortified Camo Regrow Lead.** The MOABs soak pierce meant for the DDTs.
- Monkey Intelligence Bureau, Village 2-3-0: $12,850 over your main damage
- Spike Factory 2-4-0 Spike Storm: $13,300 (camo, Lead, Purple; ability for the DDT wave)
- Dartling 0-4-0 Rocket Storm: $11,550

**R98: 30 Fortified BFB, 8 ZOMG (327,456 RBE).** Pure blimp damage and stalls.
- Sniper 4-0-2 Maim MOAB: $11,400 each; two of them chain stuns
- Bomb 2-4-0 MOAB Assassin: $6,375 (ability on each ZOMG)
- Glue 2-0-3 MOAB Glue: $5,005 slows every blimp

**R99: 60 MOAB, 9 Fortified DDT.** Arrives while your R98 abilities recharge.
- Buccaneer 0-4-2 Monkey Pirates: $6,800 (Crow's Nest lets the hook take DDTs)
- Ninja 0-4-0 Bloon Sabotage: $7,450 halves DDT speed
- Spike Factory 2-4-0: $13,300 at the exit

**R100: 1 BAD (28,000 HP outer layer).** Immune to slows, stuns and knockback.
- Sub 0-4-0 First Strike Capability: $15,425 (time it after the BAD's last damage phase)
- Spike Factory 0-4-0 Spike Storm ability: $11,900
- Bomb 0-5-0 MOAB Eliminator: $31,475, or [Ezili](/heroes/ezili/)'s MOAB Hex (L10)

## Save abilities for

**R40, 63, 75, 78, 95, 98, 99 and 100.** Don't fire them on an easy round because they're ready.

## Alternate Bloons Rounds (ABR)

ABR unlocks after Hard Standard and is played on Hard from R3 to R80. Everything arrives much earlier, and fortified blimps start at R40. Total cash is about 3% higher than Hard (calc). The budget is about the same; the threats are what change.

| ABR round | Contents | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| **5** | 12 Camo Red, 1 Camo Regrow Red, 15 Regrow Blue, 3 Green | **First camo.** Detection from the start |
| **10** | 40 Green, 22 Blue, 2 Lead | First Lead. Leads block sharp shots aimed at the Blues. A 1-0-0 Sniper, or let the Leads leak |
| **15** | 3 Purple among Blues, Greens, Yellows, Pinks | First Purple |
| **24** | 1 Camo Lead | First Camo Lead |
| **27** | 100 Camo Red, 60 Camo Blue, 45 Camo Green, 45 Camo Yellow | **Massive camo rush.** Grouped camo damage and decamo |
| **30** | 9 Camo Lead | Camo and lead on the same tower |
| **40** | 1 Fortified MOAB (400 HP) | **First blimp is fortified.** MOAB Assassin's ability one-shots it; Corvus helps |
| **45** | 10 Camo Purple, 120 Pink, 10 Ceramic, 4 Fortified Lead | First Camo Purple |
| **46** | 8 Fortified Ceramic | Single-target damage |
| **50** | 2 Fortified MOAB, 4 MOAB, 8 Fortified Regrow Lead | Single-target and area damage |
| **51** | 28 Fortified Camo Ceramic, 10 Zebra | Camo plus ceramic damage |
| **54** | 1 BFB, 1 MOAB, 35 Regrow Black | First BFB |
| **60** | 1 Fortified BFB | MOAB damage |
| **63** | 30 Fortified Ceramic, 40 Ceramic, 40 Camo Ceramic, 5 MOAB | Decamo or grouped camo popping |
| **69** | 40 Camo Regrow Purple, 40 Camo Regrow Black, 50 Camo Ceramic | All-camo round |
| **75** | 6 BFB, 2 Fortified BFB, 4 Fortified MOAB, 64 Leads (14 Fortified) | Called the hardest round before Freeplay. Save abilities |
| **78** | 60 Fortified Ceramic, 72 Camo Ceramic, 80 Purple, 150 Zebra, 1 Fortified BFB | Camo ceramic wave |
| **79** | 1 ZOMG, 4 Fortified BFB, 500 Camo Rainbow | First ZOMG |
| **80** | 2 ZOMG | ABR's final round |

Optional ABR Freeplay: first DDTs on R88, a BAD on R99, a Fortified BAD on R100. ABR rounds 101–140 are preset and harder than standard Freeplay.

**ABR openers:** Dart + [Ninja](/monkeys/ninja/), Dart + [Sub](/monkeys/sub/), or Dart + [Sniper](/monkeys/sniper/) for early camo, then save for a hero. [Benjamin](/heroes/benjamin/), [Sauda](/heroes/sauda/), [Corvus](/heroes/corvus/) and [Geraldo](/heroes/geraldo/) are popular. Monkeyopolis in the 40s–50s is the usual money plan.
