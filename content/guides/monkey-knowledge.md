# Monkey Knowledge

Take Mo' Monkey Money, Monkey Education and Elite Military Training first. All three are cheap, and they speed up every MM and XP goal after them.

## How it works

- **Unlocks at player level 30.**
- **Points:** 1 per level from 30 to 155, skipping 33 and 36 (124 points), plus achievement points. 138 in total without purchases.
- **Six trees, 134 nodes:** Primary (32), Military (30), Magic (22), Support (22), Heroes (13), Powers (15).
- Each node costs 1 point. Deeper tiers need points already spent in that tree, and tier 4+ also costs MM.
- **Respec:** 2,000 MM. Points come back; MM already paid stays unlocked.
- **Toggling:** since v57 you can switch all MK, or any single node, off and on.
- **CHIMPS ignores all MK.**

| Tier | Primary | Military | Magic | Support | Heroes | Powers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 5 pts | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 8 + 250 MM | 8 + 250 | 4 + 250 | 3 + 250 | 3 + 250 | 3 + 250 |
| 5 | 10 + 500 | 8 + 500 | 8 + 500 | 8 + 500 | 8 + 500 | 3 + 500 |
| 6+ | 10 + 1,000 | 14 + 1,000 | 8 + 1,000 | 14 + 1,000 | 8 + 1,000 | 10 + 1,000 |

"Pts" is the number of points you must already have spent in that tree.

## Beginner priority list

### Phase 1: cheap and immediate

| # | MK | Tree | Pts / MM | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lingering Magic → Speedy Brewing → **Mo' Monkey Money** | Magic | 0 / 0 | +10% MM from wins and Daily Challenges |
| 2 | **Monkey Education** | Support | 3 / 0 | All towers and heroes earn 8% more XP |
| 3 | **Elite Military Training** | Military | 0 / 0 | +1,000 XP once to every Military tower, and +5% Military XP |
| 4 | Self Taught Heroes | Heroes | 3 / 0 | Heroes earn XP 10% faster |
| 4 | Hero Favors | Heroes | 3 / 250 | Heroes 10% cheaper |
| 5 | Extra Dart Pops, Naval Upgrades | Primary / Military | 0 / 0 | +1 pierce to Darts; +1 pierce to Subs and Buccaneers |
| 5 | Farm Subsidy, First Last Line of Defense | Support | – | Small discounts on your first Farm and first Spike Factory |

To reach Monkey Education, spend 3 points on cheap Support nodes such as Flat Pack Buildings (Farms and Villages 2% cheaper, sell for 2% more), Insider Trades or More Valuable Bananas.

### Phase 2: economy and defence

| MK | Tree | Pts / MM | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **More Cash** | Primary | 10 / 1,000 | +$200 starting cash |
| **Bonus Monkey!** or Bonus Glue Gunner | Primary | 10 / 1,000 | A free Dart or Glue at the start (one free tower per game) |
| **Better Sell Deals** | Support | 8 / 500 | All towers sell for 5% more |
| **Veteran Monkey Training** | Support | 8 / 500 | All towers reload 3% faster |
| **Global Ability Cooldowns** | Support | 8 / 500 | Ability cooldowns −3% |
| **Healthy Bananas** | Support | 8 / 500 | Marketplace +1 life per round, Central Market +3 |
| **Empowered Heroes** | Heroes | 8 / 500 | Heroes start at level 3 |
| **Mana Shield** | Magic | 8 / 1,000 | Absorbs up to 25 lives, slowly recharges |
| There Can Be Only One | Magic | 8 / 500 | In-game text is only "There can be only one." (effect unverified) |

### Phase 3: deep and Paragon-related

| MK | Tree | Pts / MM | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Big Bloon Sabotage** | Military | 14 / 1,000 | MOAB-class spawn partly damaged. A must for Freeplay |
| **Military Conscription** | Military | 14 / 1,000 | First Military tower each game costs two-thirds |
| **Bank Deposits** | Support | 14 / 1,000 | Tier 4+ Monkey Banks accept deposits |
| **Paragon of Power** | Support | 14 / 1,000 | Paragons attack faster with no same-type tier 5 on the map. Almost essential for Boss Events |
| Pre-game Prep | Powers | 3 / 250 | Free permanent Road Spikes at the end of the track |

The Powers tree has the least impact.

## Economy and XP knowledge

| MK | Effect |
|---|---|
| Monkey Education | +8% tower and hero XP |
| Elite Military Training | +1,000 one-off and +5% Military XP |
| Self Taught Heroes | +10% hero XP |
| Monkeys Together Strong | +5% hero XP per hero placed |
| Mo' Monkey Money | +10% MM on wins |
| More Valuable Bananas | Valuable Bananas +30% instead of +25% (BRF $1,875 → $1,950 a round) |
| Bigger Banks | +$2,500 bank capacity |
| Backroom Deals | IMF Loan +$1,000, repaid at 40% |
| Trade Agreements | Merchantman +$20 a round |
| Inland Revenue Streams | Monkey Town +50% → +60% pop cash |
| Grand Prix Spree | Race entry −10% MM |

There is no MK called "Bigger Banana Farms".

## Worth toggling off

| MK | Why |
|---|---|
| Emergency Unlock | Can block ability hotkeys |
| Cross the Streams | Freeplay lag |
| Acid Stability | Freeplay lag, and it steals Rubber to Gold income |
| Targeted Pineapples | Can lower the pineapple rate on some maps |
| Big Bloon Sabotage | While farming Paragon Degree in Boss Events |

Elite Military Training's +1,000 is not re-granted after a respec.
