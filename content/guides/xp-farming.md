# Tower XP Farming

Expert Deflation is the best farm for almost every tower. It starts on round 31 with $20,000, needs no income, and pays 59,735 base XP in 30 rounds. Put nearly all of that $20,000 into the tower you're levelling.

## How tower XP works

1. **XP is paid per completed round.** Pops don't count. Round 20 gives 420, round 50 gives 1,620, round 100 gives 6,120.
2. **It is split by cash invested.** At the end of each round, the round's XP is shared among your placed towers in proportion to the money in them, upgrades included.
3. **Heroes take no share.** Hero spending is ignored, so a hero is a free helper for XP purposes.
4. **Unplaced towers get nothing.** The only exception is Elite Military Training's one-off +1,000 to every Military tower.
5. **The map tier multiplies XP. The difficulty does not.** Difficulty only changes which rounds you play.

Example: $19,000 of Ninjas and $1,000 of Darts means Ninja gets 95% of each round's XP and Dart gets 5%.

### XP per round

| Rounds | XP for round r | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1–20 | 20r + 20 | R1 = 40, R20 = 420 |
| 21–50 | 40(r − 20) + 420 | R30 = 820, R50 = 1,620 |
| 51+ | 90(r − 50) + 1,620 | R60 = 2,520, R80 = 4,320, R100 = 6,120 |

### Multipliers

| Source | Effect |
|---|---|
| Beginner map | ×1.0 |
| Intermediate map | ×1.1 |
| Advanced map | ×1.2 |
| Expert map | ×1.3 |
| Monkey Education (MK) | +8% tower and hero XP |
| Elite Military Training (MK) | +5% for Military towers, plus +1,000 once to each |
| Freeplay, after the win, up to R100 | XP cut by 70% (you get 30%) |
| Freeplay, R101 onward | XP cut by 90% (you get 10%) |

- Impoppable and CHIMPS rounds up to 100 are not Freeplay. They pay in full.
- MK is off in CHIMPS, so CHIMPS runs use the no-MK numbers.
- The map multiplier and the MK bonuses are assumed to stack by multiplying (unverified). Adding them instead changes results by about 0.4%.
- Elite Military Training applies to Sniper, Sub, Buccaneer, Ace, Heli, Mortar and Dartling. [Desperado](/monkeys/desperado/) is a Primary tower, so it gets no EMT bonus.
- Sandbox gives no XP (community knowledge, unverified).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 1–40 | 21,400 | 27,820 | ≈30,000 |
| Medium | 1–60 | 56,950 | 74,035 | ≈80,000 |
| **Deflation** | **31–60** | **45,950** | **59,735** | **64,514** |
| Hard | 3–80 | 126,150 | 163,995 | 177,115 |
| Impoppable / CHIMPS | 6–100 | 231,150 | 300,495 | 324,535 (Impoppable only) |

(calc) Freeplay is a poor deal. Rounds 81–100 played after a Hard win pay 30% of 105,300 = 31,590. Rounds 101–150 pay only 42,075 in total. A fresh game is faster.

| Method | XP per hour (calc) |
|---|---|
| Deflation | ≈322,600 |

Impoppable only wins at a 100% share, which means an all-target defence from R6 to R100. Few towers can do that. At realistic shares (Deflation 85–100%, Impoppable 30–55%), Deflation wins for 25 of 26 towers. It also runs on fast-forward with little attention.

### The methods

**A. Expert Deflation.** Unlocks on a map after Primary Only. Start R31 with $20,000 ($20,200 with More Cash), no income at all, Easy prices (×0.85), ends after R60. Buy the whole defence before R31 and fast-forward. Dark Castle and Infernal are the usual maps.

**B. Impoppable or CHIMPS to R100 on Expert.** The most XP per game (≈300k) plus a round-100 Insta Monkey and MM. The defence takes an XP share unless it is all the target tower. Best for towers that can carry a whole game alone: Ninja, Ace, Sub, Buccaneer, Engineer, Druid.

**C. Hard to R80 on Expert.** About 164k base XP. A middle step while you learn.

**D. Stack XP onto other goals.** Medal runs, Odyssey islands, Races, Boss Events and Daily Challenges all pay round XP. Use the tower you're levelling.

**Skip:** the "play one round, lose, restart" trick. Rounds 1–2 give only 40–60 XP.

### Keeping your share high

- Share = target spend ÷ (target spend + helper spend). A $1,405 helper next to $18,000 of target gives 92.8%.
- Cover the target's weak spot with the **hero** first, since it costs no share: [Gwendolin](/heroes/gwendolin/) for lead, [Etienne](/heroes/etienne/) for camo from level 8, [Ezili](/heroes/ezili/) for purple, [Striker Jones](/heroes/striker/) for black.
- Two towers you both want can share one run (Alchemist + Ninja, Village + Dart). The total is the same, just divided.
- Placed Insta Monkeys cost no cash, so they probably take no share (unverified).

**Monkey Commerce trick:** place a 0-0-2 Village first ($1,870 Easy), buy your tier 1–3 upgrades inside it for 15% off, then sell it before R31. It takes no XP once sold. It pays for itself only above about $3,740 of tier 0–3 purchases (calc). It does nothing for tier 4–5 upgrades.

## Worked example: Sniper Monkey to S.P.I.D.E.R. Monkey

[Sniper Monkey](/monkeys/sniper/) needs 145,200 XP for all 15 upgrades and 750,000 more to unlock S.P.I.D.E.R. Monkey: **895,200 in total**, or 894,200 after Elite Military Training's one-off +1,000.

### The farm: Expert Deflation on Infernal

[Infernal](/maps/infernal/) has the longest Expert lanes: 19.3 RBS each (Glacial Trail is next at 17.7). The two lanes alternate: odd rounds come down one, even rounds the other. Snipers need line of sight, and the BTD6 Index flags no line-of-sight blockers on Infernal. Elite Sniper and Elite Defender have each beaten CHIMPS alone on this map.

| Unit | Build | Count | Easy price each | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sniper Monkey (target) | 2-0-4 Full Auto Rifle | 2 | $8,240 | $16,480 |
| Monkey Village (helper) | 0-2-0 (Grow Blocker, Radar Scanner) | 1 | $2,930 | $2,930 |
| Hero | [Quincy](/heroes/quincy/) | 1 | $460 | $460 |
| **Total** | | | | **$19,870 of $20,000** |

Why it works:
- **Camo:** a 2-0-4 has no room for Night Vision Goggles. The Village's Radar Scanner gives camo detection to every tower in its radius, and Snipers have infinite range, so both lanes are covered every round. That handles all 11 camo rounds from R33 to R59, including the R59 Camo Leads.
- **Lead and Frozen:** Full Metal Jacket (1-x-x).
- **Regrow:** Grow Blocker.
- **Blimps:** two Full Auto Rifles deal about 139 damage per second to MOAB-class (calc).
- **Placement:** both Snipers and the Village together on the right-side island, so both sit inside the camo circle (untested).

The numbers (calc):

| | No MK | With ME + EMT |
|---|---|---|
| Sniper share | 16,480 ÷ 19,410 = 84.9% | 84.9% |
| XP per run | 59,735 × 0.849 = **50,718** | 67,739 × 0.849 = **57,514** |
| Runs to all 15 upgrades | 145,200 ÷ 50,718 → **3** | 144,200 ÷ 57,514 → **3** |
| Runs to S.P.I.D.E.R. Monkey | 895,200 ÷ 50,718 → **18** | 894,200 ÷ 57,514 → **16** |

**Main risk:** single-target damage on the R49 and R54–55 ceramic rushes. Leak size isn't simulated, though Deflation's 200 lives absorb some. Send R49–R60 in Sandbox before your first run.

### Variant: Etienne instead of Quincy

- Same towers with [Etienne](/heroes/etienne/) ($555 Easy). Total $19,965.
- At level 8, Etienne's UAV gives every tower camo detection. **Only once his panel shows level 8**, sell the Village (about $2,050 back) and buy a third Sniper at 1-0-2 ($1,355 Easy).
- Level 8 needs 20,280 hero XP, which is around the end of R48 if heroes earn round XP at ×1.0 from R31 (unverified).
- XP: 84.9% share for R31–48, 100% for R49–60. That gives **55,496 XP per run** without MK, 62,933 with MK (calc). 17 runs to the Paragon without MK, 15 with.

### What not to do

- **Mortar Signal Flare for camo.** One fixed reticle can't follow alternating lanes. A full leak of R56's 40 Camo Rainbows costs 1,880 lives against Deflation's 200 (lives unverified).
- **All-Sniper with no Village.** It dies to the R59 Camo Leads.
- **Elite Defender alone.** It can't pop Lead.

### Faster route: mono-Sniper on Hard

Once the Deflation farm is stable and the x-4-x and x-x-4 upgrades are unlocked, an all-Sniper defence on Expert Hard puts 100% of a much bigger XP pool into Snipers. Supply Drop (0-4-0) income is allowed on Hard and funds more Snipers.

| Farm (Infernal, with ME + EMT) | Sniper XP per run | Runs to S.P.I.D.E.R. Monkey from zero |
|---|---|---|
| Deflation R31–60, build above (84.9%) | 57,514 | 16 |
| **Hard R3–80, Snipers only (100%)** | **185,970** | **5** |
| Impoppable R6–100, Snipers only (100%) | 340,761 | 3 |

- Without MK, Hard pays 163,995 per run: 6 runs to the Paragon (calc).
- A Hard run takes roughly 3× as long as a Deflation run but pays about 3.2× the XP, plus MM and medals.
- Whether you keep the XP from finished rounds if you lose partway is unconfirmed.
- These are calculations. None of the mono-Sniper runs has been tested in game.
