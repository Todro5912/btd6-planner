# Game Modes

Each mode unlocks the next on the same map. Sub-modes use their parent difficulty's prices, with one exception: Impoppable has its own ×1.2.

- **Starting cash** with the More Cash MK: +$200 ($850), $425 in Half Cash, $20,200 in Deflation, unchanged in CHIMPS.
- **Sell rate** is 70% everywhere selling is allowed. The Lych boss halves it to 35% while on screen.
- **End-of-round cash** is $100 + the round number in every mode except Deflation.
- **Pop cash** falls to 50% at R51, 20% at R61, 10% at R86, 8% at R101, 5% at R121 and 2% at R141.

## Notes per mode

**Primary Only.** Dart, Boomerang, Bomb, Tack, Ice, Glue and Desperado, plus heroes. Round-100 Instas from this mode are always Primary towers.

**Deflation.** Start R31 with $20,000. Popping and end-of-round cash are both off. Spend everything before R31; buy a Monkey Commerce first and build inside it, then sell it. Three Mines 'Round crystals still pay out, and since v57 Bloon Traps and Obyn's Wall of Trees generate cash here. Whether Easy's R40 MOAB HP cut (200 → 133) applies here is unverified. The best tower XP farm in the game: see [XP Farming](/guides/xp-farming/).

**Military Only.** Sniper, Sub, Buccaneer, Ace, Heli, Mortar and Dartling, plus heroes.

**Apopalypse.** Randomly generated rounds. The next round spawns as soon as the last one finishes spawning, so there are no breaks. Strong bloons arrive early: Camo from about R19, Leads from R20, Ceramics from R33, MOABs from R41. Ground objects such as spikes stay between rounds. The win round is disputed: one source says R60, an undated update note says "Completing round 70 now counts as beating the mode" (unverified).

**Reverse.** Barely changes most maps. Makes [Dark Castle](/maps/dark-castle/) and [Workshop](/maps/workshop/) harder.

**Magic Monkeys Only.** Wizard, Super Monkey, Ninja, Alchemist, Druid, Mermonkey and Skywarden, plus heroes.

**Double HP MOABs.** Stacks with fortified and the R81+ ramp. Only slightly harder than Hard overall, but the R40 MOAB (400 HP) becomes a real wall.

**Half Cash.** Starting cash, pop cash, end-of-round cash, continue cash and tower income are halved. Not halved: Monkey Bank interest, Geraldo's Rare Quincy Action Figure, Corvus's Nourishment and Three Mines 'Round crystals. Banks are the best farm here. Often called the most frustrating mode.

**Alternate Bloons Rounds.** Camo from R5, Lead from R10, Purple from R15, a Fortified MOAB on R40, first BFB R54, first ZOMG R79, two ZOMGs on R80. See [Danger Rounds](/guides/danger-rounds/).

**Impoppable.** Standard rounds 6–100, 1 life, ×1.2 prices (about 11% above Hard). MK, Powers, Instas, farms and Continues are allowed. Freeplay-style modifiers apply from R81 as in every mode that reaches it.

**CHIMPS.** Hard prices (×1.08), cheaper than Impoppable's ×1.2. Continues are replaced by Retry Last Round. Red medal for any win, black for one clean session. Full rules on the [CHIMPS](/guides/chimps/) page.

**Sandbox.** Unlocks per difficulty: Easy after Easy Standard, Medium after Reverse, Hard after Hard Standard. Set any round, send any bloon, reset cooldowns. Paragons and the Vengeful True Sun God appear only after you've built them in a real game. No Co-op. No XP (community knowledge, unverified).

## Freeplay

After a win you can keep playing. From R81 (inside the normal game for Impoppable and CHIMPS):
- Ceramics become Super Ceramics.
- Bloons gain speed each round, and MOAB-class HP ramps up.
- Lives lost per leak go down.
- Stuns and slows get shorter from R150.

Freeplay XP is cut by 70% up to R100 and by 90% after. There's no extra MM, but you get an Insta Monkey at R200, R300 and so on.
