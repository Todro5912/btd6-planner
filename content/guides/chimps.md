# CHIMPS

CHIMPS is Hard prices (×1.08), 1 life, rounds 6–100, $650 to start, and no farms, Monkey Knowledge, Powers or selling. You get about $178.7k over the whole game. Every dollar has to sit in a tower that still works on round 100.

## The rules

CHIMPS stands for **no Continues, Hearts lost, Income, Monkey Knowledge, Powers or Selling.** It unlocks on a map after you beat Impoppable there.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Prices | Hard (×1.08). Impoppable is ×1.2 |
| Rounds | 6–100, standard round set |
| Lives | 1, and you can never gain more. Odyssey uses its own life pool |
| Starting cash | $650. More Cash MK does not apply |
| Continues | Off. Replaced by **Retry Last Round** |
| Income | Only starting cash, pop cash and end-of-round cash. Banana Farms are disabled even if a challenge allows them. Double Cash is off |
| Traps | Bloon Trap, XXXL Trap and Obyn's Wall of Trees pay only what the bloons would have paid |
| Map gimmicks | Cash-paying gimmicks still work, such as the Three Mines 'Round crystals |
| Monkey Knowledge | None of it applies |
| Powers | Off, including Insta Monkeys. Hero Boosters, Fast Track and Perks are also off |
| Selling | Not allowed. An indirect sale (a Support Chinook moving an Arctic Wind, for example) returns $0 |
| Sell exceptions | Champion Sentries, Paragon Sentries, Geraldo's level 18+ Creepy Idol and the Special Poperations Marine can still be sold |
| Sacrifices | Adora's Blood Sacrifice and Sun Temple sacrifices don't count as selling |
| Rubber to Gold | Pays nothing since 57.1 |
| Lead to Gold | Pays +$50 per Lead it pops, since 57.0. 57.1 only removed the 0-0-4 gold mutator's income |

### Medals

- **Red CHIMPS:** beat the map on CHIMPS at all, even using Retry Last Round.
- **Black CHIMPS:** beat it in one session, without exiting the game and without Retry Last Round. You can come back later just for black.
- In Co-op, a player who quits and rejoins gets only red. The others can still get black.
- The black medal plus every other medal on the map gives the **black border**.

Retry Last Round makes red feel easier than Impoppable. Black is the real test.

## Cash checkpoints

Cash entering each round with no income towers (calc). "Med-eq" is what it buys at Medium prices (÷ 1.08).

| Entering | Cash | Med-eq | What it has to cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| R24 | $5,059 | $4,684 | Opener, hero, first camo |
| R28 | $6,556 | $6,070 | Lead popping |
| R40 | $16,250 | $15,046 | MOAB answer plus camo and lead |
| R50 | $34,559 | $31,999 | Mid-game carry |
| R63 | $57,017 | $52,794 | Ceramic rush. Only 32% of the game's money has arrived |
| R80 | $96,118 | $88,999 | Win condition in place |
| R85 | $119,435 | $110,588 | Last rounds at 20% pop cash |
| R90 | $131,121 | $121,408 | DDT answer |
| R95 | $149,802 | $138,706 | R95 DDTs and Purples |
| R98 | $164,893 | $152,679 | Highest-RBE round |
| R100 | $177,375 | $164,236 | The BAD |

Rules of thumb:
- About 68% of the game's money arrives from R63 onward. The late game is where your cash is.
- Pop cash drops to 10% at R86. **Buy an expensive win condition in the early 80s, before R85.**
- A $100k (CHIMPS price) win condition bought before R85 leaves only about $19k for everything else (calc).
- A 3-2-0 Sun Avatar costs $32,830 in CHIMPS. Entering R50 you have $34,559, which leaves $1,729 for everything else. Plan tier 5s for R60–80.
- One sourced no-micro [Monkey Meadow](/maps/monkey-meadow/) win finishes with about $91k unspent, so efficient routes use roughly half the available cash.

## How to prepare

1. **Beat Impoppable on the map first.** You need it to unlock CHIMPS anyway, and it teaches the map.
2. **Write the plan by round.** Use the [Danger Rounds](/guides/danger-rounds/) list and the cash table above. The [Planner](/planner/) prices each purchase at CHIMPS prices and places it in the first round you can afford it.
3. **Test it in Sandbox.** Send R40, R63, R95, R98, R99 and R100 against your build.
4. **Go for red first.** Use Retry Last Round freely to learn the weak rounds.
5. **Come back for black** in one clean session.

### Phase plan

**R6–40**
- An opener: 3× 0-0-0 [Dart Monkey](/monkeys/dart/) (one on Strong), or Dart + [Sniper](/monkeys/sniper/), Dart + [Sub](/monkeys/sub/) on water, or a [Ninja](/monkeys/ninja/) 1-0-0 into 1-0-1.
- Hero by about R15.
- Camo by R24, lead by R28.
- A MOAB answer for R40: Destroyer (Buccaneer 3-1-0), MOAB Mauler (Bomb x-3-x), Airburst Darts Sub with Berserker Brew into Triple Guns, Ezili at level 6, or Corvus.

**R41–80**
- A mid-game carry: Bloon Area Denial System (Dartling 0-0-4), Artillery Battery, Comanche Defense or Plasma Accelerator.
- A win condition by R80: Carrier Flagship, Glaive Lord, Spiked Mines or Lord of the Abyss.

**R81–100**
- Supports: **Jungle Drums (always)**, Giant Condor, Berserker Brew or Stronger Stimulant, Relentless Glue into Super Glue, MOAB Press 2-0-4, Bloon Sabotage, Glue Strike 1-4-0.
- Situational: Monkey Intelligence Bureau, Embrittlement or Glue Storm, Overclock, Shattering Shells.
- A second win condition is fine if both together cost under $100k.
- Keep Spike Storm or First Strike Capability for R100.

### Win conditions with crosspaths

| Win condition | Build | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Perma-Spike | Spike Factory 1-0-5 | Leak tank. Pair with Jungle Drums and a 3-0-1 Berserker Brew Alchemist |
| Glaive Lord | Boomerang 5-2-0 or 5-0-2 | Best on bends. 5-2-0 if you need lead or MIB |
| Carrier Flagship | Buccaneer 5-2-0 or 5-0-2 | With [Admiral Brickell](/heroes/brickell/). Solos the mid game |
| The Bloon Solver | Glue 5-2-0 | With Carpet of Spikes |
| Bloon Exclusion Zone | Dartling 2-0-5 | With Stronger Stimulant and camo. A 2-0-4 saves toward it |
| Comanche Commander | Heli 2-0-5 | With [Geraldo](/heroes/geraldo/) and Glue Storm, single-lane maps |
| Pop and Awe | Mortar x-5-x | With [Striker Jones](/heroes/striker/), stuns, Perma-Spike and a BAD answer |

**Avoid in CHIMPS:** Rubber to Gold, Bloon Master Alchemist, [Benjamin](/heroes/benjamin/), Merchantman / Trade Empire and Monkey City. They are income or economy tools that do nothing here.

### Example route: Monkey Meadow, no micro

A community route with [Gwendolin](/heroes/gwendolin/), no abilities, about $91k left over.

| Round | Buy |
|---|---|
| 6 | Tack Shooter 1-0-1 |
| 8 | Tack 1-0-2, then 2-0-2 |
| 13 | Gwendolin next to the Tack |
| 19 | Spike Factory 0-0-2 (end of round) |
| 34–35 | Spike Factory 0-0-4 Deadly Spikes, then 0-2-4 |
| 39 | Tack 2-0-4 Overdrive (the source also says 0-2-4; unverified which) |
| 41 | Alchemist 3-0-0 Berserker Brew on the spikes |
| 44–45 | Village 0-2-0, then 3-2-0 (Primary Training + Radar Scanner) |
| 49 | Alchemist to 4-0-1 Stronger Stimulant, close to the Tack |
| 59 | Tack 2-0-5 The Tack Zone |

The Tack Zone is the carry, buffed by Primary Training and Gwendolin. Perma-Spike catches leaks.

## Best maps for a first CHIMPS

Long, single-lane maps with lots of track overlap.

| Tier | Maps |
|---|---|
| Beginner | [Logs](/maps/logs/) (the usual first pick), [Resort](/maps/resort/), [Candy Falls](/maps/candy-falls/), [Spa Pits](/maps/spa-pits/), [Three Mines 'Round](/maps/three-mines-round/), [Cubism](/maps/cubism/), [Four Circles](/maps/four-circles/), [Monkey Meadow](/maps/monkey-meadow/), [Winter Park](/maps/winter-park/), [Town Center](/maps/town-center/), [Park Path](/maps/park-path/) |
| Intermediate | [Sulfur Springs](/maps/sulfur-springs/), [KartsNDarts](/maps/kartsndarts/), [Cracked](/maps/cracked/), [Streambed](/maps/streambed/) |
| Advanced | [Off The Coast](/maps/off-the-coast/), [Cornfield](/maps/cornfield/) |
| Expert | [Infernal](/maps/infernal/), then [Dark Castle](/maps/dark-castle/) |

Leave [Bloody Puddles](/maps/bloody-puddles/), [Ravine](/maps/ravine/), [Quad](/maps/quad/), [#Ouch](/maps/ouch/), [Dark Dungeons](/maps/dark-dungeons/), [Muddy Puddles](/maps/muddy-puddles/), [X Factor](/maps/x-factor/) and [Geared](/maps/geared/) for later. [Erosion](/maps/erosion/) is brutal in CHIMPS: its collapsing land sells your towers for $0.
