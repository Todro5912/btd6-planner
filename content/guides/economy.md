# Economy and Income

Money earned before round 50 is the most valuable money in the game. Pop cash halves at R51 and keeps falling, while farm income never shrinks. So farms bought early pay off, and farms bought late often don't.

All prices are Medium unless a column says otherwise. **(calc)** marks derived numbers.

## Where cash comes from

- **Starting cash:** $650 in most modes, including Impoppable and CHIMPS. Half Cash starts with $325. Deflation starts on R31 with $20,000 and pays nothing after that. More Cash MK adds $200 ($100 in Half Cash, $200 in Deflation, nothing in CHIMPS).
- **End-of-round bonus:** $100 + the round number. Round 1 pays $101, round 100 pays $200.
- **Pop cash:** about $1 per layer popped, cut in later rounds:

| Rounds | Pop cash |
|---|---|
| 1–50 | 100% |
| 51–60 | 50% |
| 61–85 | 20% |
| 86–100 | 10% |
| 101–120 | 5% |
| 121–140 | 4% |
| 141+ | 2% |

What the cut means (calc): rounds 1–50 pay about $31k of pop cash for 33k RBE. Rounds 86–100 throw 1.48 million RBE at you and pay about $54k. A Ceramic popped on R40 is worth about 10× one popped on R90.

### When to stop buying farms

A farm needs about 15 rounds to repay itself. So stop buying farms about 15 rounds before the end: around R25 on Easy, R45 on Medium, R65 on Hard and R85 on Impoppable (calc). If you'll sell the farm into a tier 5 later, you only need to cover the 30% you lose on resale, so you can keep buying until about 5 rounds before you sell.

Farm income doesn't scale with difficulty, but prices do. Farms pay back 8% slower on Hard, 20% slower on Impoppable and more than twice as slowly in Half Cash. On Easy they pay back 15% faster.

**Half Cash:** Monkey Bank interest is not halved. A 2-3-0 bank averages about $470 per round there against a Marketplace's $200, which makes banks the best farm in that mode (calc).

## CHIMPS: what's legal

CHIMPS income is starting cash, pop cash and end-of-round cash. **Nothing in the income table above makes money in CHIMPS.**

| Source | CHIMPS |
|---|---|
| Banana Farms | Disabled |
| Benjamin, Merchantmen, Supply Drop, Jungle's Bounty | No income |
| Rubber to Gold | Pays nothing since 57.1 |
| Lead to Gold | Conflicting reports; plan as if it pays nothing (unverified) |
| Bloon Trap, XXXL Trap, Obyn's Wall of Trees | Only what the bloons would pay if popped normally |
| Monkey Town / City / Monkeyopolis | No extra income |
| Map gimmicks that pay cash (Three Mines 'Round crystals) | Still pay |
| Double Cash | Off |

## Discounts

**Monkey Village bottom path:**
- **Monkey Business (x-x-1, $500):** −10% on towers and on upgrades of tier 3 or less bought in its radius. +2% with Insider Trades MK.
- **Monkey Commerce (x-x-2, $500):** another −5%. Up to 3 Commerce Villages stack, for a maximum of 25% off.
- Tier 4 and 5 upgrades, hero levels and Geraldo's items are not discounted. Paragons can't be discounted.

Break-even (Medium $ of tier 0–3 buying in range, calc):

| Setup | Discount | Break-even if kept | If sold after (70% back) |
|---|---|---|---|
| One 0-0-1 | 10% | $17,000 | $5,100 |
| One 0-0-2 | 15% | $14,667 | $4,400 |
| Three 0-0-2 | 25% | $23,320 | $6,996 |

Two discount Villages are never the best choice: by the time two beat one, three already beat two. In CHIMPS you can't sell, so use the "if kept" column.

**Monkeyopolis trick** (outside CHIMPS): buy 3 Monkey Commerce Villages next to each other, buy a Monkey City inside all three, sell one Commerce, buy a 0-0-0 Farm inside the other two, sell the other two, then upgrade to Monkeyopolis.

## Difficulty prices

| Difficulty | Price multiplier | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | ×0.85 | Easy, Primary Only, **Deflation** |
| Medium | ×1.0 | Medium, Military Only, Apopalypse, Reverse |
| Hard | ×1.08 | Hard, Magic Monkeys Only, Double HP MOABs, Half Cash, ABR, **CHIMPS** |
| Impoppable | ×1.2 | Impoppable only |

**The $5 rounding rule.** Each base price and each upgrade is multiplied, then rounded to the nearest $5 on its own. A tower's total is the sum of those rounded prices.

```
price = round_to_5(medium_price × multiplier)    per upgrade
ties ($2.50 over a multiple of $5) round DOWN
```

- Ties only happen on Easy, when the Medium price is an odd multiple of $50. Examples: Monkey Bank $3,650 → $3,100, Banana Farm $1,250 → $1,060, EZ Collect $250 → $210, Crow's Nest $350 → $295.
- Hard and Impoppable can never tie (calc).
- Base tower prices may not follow the tie rule: Sniper $350 → $300 rounds up on Easy (unverified).
- Multiplying a whole tower's Medium total instead can be off by $5–10.
