About

Monkey Intel by Solou

An unofficial Bloons TD 6 fan project, made by Todro5912 for Solou.

Source on GitHub Report an error

What this is

  • Fast answers for when you're in a game: what pops camo, lead, purple and MOABs, and the cheapest fix on your difficulty.
  • A page for every tower, hero, map and round 1–100.
  • Tools for between games: the simulator, XP calculator and defence check.
  • Unofficial. Not made, checked or endorsed by Ninja Kiwi. No game art is used.

How it's made

  • Todro5912 is a BTD6 player and the developer. The site is built with AI assistance: research, code and copy are drafted with an AI model, then checked and edited by Todro5912.
  • Numbers come from the sources below for game version 57.4.
  • Anything worked out rather than read from a source carries a calculated badge. Anything not yet confirmed carries unverified.

Credits

  • Made by Todro5912, for Solou.
  • Bloons Wiki: stats, prices, XP and map facts (CC BY-SA).
  • BTD6 Fandom wiki: cross-checks.
  • BTD6 Index: lane lengths, water and line-of-sight flags, CHIMPS records.
  • Cyber Quincy: cross-checks for costs and rounds.
  • Ninja Kiwi patch notes: balance changes.
  • Fonts: Luckiest Guy, Rubik and JetBrains Mono (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License).

Found a mistake?

  1. Open an issue on GitHub.
  2. Say which page, what it says, and what the game shows.
  3. A screenshot from Sandbox mode settles most questions fast.

Things we already know are shaky are on Known issues.
