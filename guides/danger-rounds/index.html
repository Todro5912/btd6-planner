Danger Rounds

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05

The rounds 1–100 that end runs, what each one needs, and how Alternate Bloons Rounds move the threats earlier.

On this page

  1. Standard rounds 1–100
  2. Save abilities for
  3. Alternate Bloons Rounds (ABR)
  4. Quick threat table

Have camo detection by R24, lead popping by R28 and a MOAB answer before R40 starts. After that the big checks are R63 (ceramic rush), R78 (camo ceramics), R90 and R95 (DDTs), R98 (highest RBE) and R100 (the BAD).

Cash figures are cumulative pop cash plus end-of-round bonus from R1, without the $650 start (calc). Hard starts at R3 and Impoppable / CHIMPS at R6, so subtract about $260 or $900.

DDT answers

A DDT has Camo, Lead and Black properties: you need camo detection, and sharp and explosive damage can't hurt it.

Approach Options
Camo detection Radar Scanner (Village 0-2-0), Etienne
Decamo Submerge and Support (Sub 3-0-0), Signal Flare (Mortar 0-0-3)
Lead damage for nearby towers Acidic Mixture Dip (Alchemist 2-0-0), Gwendolin's Heat It Up
Remove lead immunity Glue Strike, Glue Storm, Embrittlement, Super Brittle
Remove black immunity Striker Jones level 5 and 19
Everything at once Monkey Intelligence Bureau (Village 0-3-0), reliable but pricey
Slow them Bloon Sabotage (Ninja 0-4-0) halves DDT speed

Effects that only reach "up to BFB" don't work on DDTs, such as Abyssal Warrior's ink and Psi before level 20.

The BAD on R100

  • Single-target bursts: Spike Storm's ability, First Strike Capability timed after the BAD's last damage phase so it one-shots the insides, MOAB Eliminator, Legend of the Night.
  • Ezili's MOAB Hex deals percentage damage.
  • Plan R98 → R99 → R100 as one sequence. Most players fire everything on R98 and have nothing for the Fortified DDTs on R99.

Save abilities for

R40, 63, 75, 78, 95, 98, 99 and 100. Don't fire them on an easy round because they're ready.

Alternate Bloons Rounds (ABR)

ABR unlocks after Hard Standard and is played on Hard from R3 to R80. Everything arrives much earlier, and fortified blimps start at R40. Total cash is about 3% higher than Hard (calc). The budget is about the same; the threats are what change.

Optional ABR Freeplay: first DDTs on R88, a BAD on R99, a Fortified BAD on R100. ABR rounds 101–140 are preset and harder than standard Freeplay.

ABR openers: Dart + Ninja, Dart + Sub, or Dart + Sniper for early camo, then save for a hero. Benjamin, Sauda, Corvus and Geraldo are popular. Monkeyopolis in the 40s–50s is the usual money plan.
