Paragons

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05

All 14 Paragons with cost and XP, how to build one, how Degree works, and which to aim for first.

On this page

  1. All 14 Paragons
  2. How to build one
  3. Degree basics
  4. Which to aim for first

A Paragon is a sixth upgrade tier that merges a tower's three tier 5s into one. Start with Apex Plasma Master: it has the cheapest full build (about $245k on Medium) and the smallest XP bar (622,300 from zero).

Paragons: - deal large bonus damage to Boss Bloons; - can't be discounted; - can only be buffed by other Paragons, Monkey Knowledge and most Perks; - ignore Powers, including Time Stop; - can't be sacrificed to a Sun Temple, True Sun God or Adora.

You can have one of each Paragon at a time, even in Co-op. Boss Events usually cap each player at 4.

All 14 Paragons

Costs are Medium. "Full build" is all three tier-5 towers plus the Paragon, before discounts. "XP from zero" is all 15 normal upgrades plus the Paragon unlock.

S.P.I.D.E.R. Monkey (v57)

  • Built from Cripple MOAB (5-0-0), Elite Sniper (0-5-0) and Elite Defender (0-0-5).
  • Main attack: a hitscan bullet every 7.5s at Degree 1, 500 damage +24,000 to MOAB-class. It bounces to a second target and releases stunning shrapnel.
  • Attack speed ramps up like Elite Defender's, from bloon progress and lives lost.
  • Ammo Swap: a 12s buff to itself and the 5 nearest Shrapnel Snipers. 90s cooldown, once per round.
  • Single Out: a charged shot that pops the strongest bloon or skips a boss skull. 180s cooldown, up to 3 times per round.
  • Other Snipers gain a "Use S.P.I.D.E.R. Monkey Target" option.
  • Nerfed and fixed in 57.1 and 57.3: bullet bonus boss damage +15,500 → +500, shrapnel pierce 100 → 1.
  • Full-map range like other Snipers. Whether it ignores line-of-sight blockers is unverified. Normal Snipers are blocked, so plan for a clear sightline.

The XP Farming page has a worked farm from zero to S.P.I.D.E.R. Monkey.

How to build one

  1. Unlock it with tower XP. Every other upgrade of that tower must be unlocked first.
  2. Have all three tier 5s on the map at once. Example for Dart: Ultra-Juggernaut, Plasma Monkey Fan Club and Crossbow Master.
  3. Click Paragon on any of the three. - A cash slider lets you put extra money in. - The Paragon absorbs every other tower of that type on the map, including Co-op partners' towers, plus every Paragon Power Totem.
  4. Sandbox only offers a Paragon after you've built it once in a real game. The Challenge Editor has no such limit.

Selling: a Paragon's sell value depends only on what was spent on the tier 5 you clicked. Upgrade from the most expensive tier 5 to get more back.

What Degree gives: - Damage: +1% per Degree above 1, plus a flat +1 per 10 Degrees. Degree 100 = 2× damage +10. - Damage bonuses (MOAB, Ceramic, Boss): +1% per Degree. - Pierce: +1% plus 0.1 flat per Degree. - Attack and ability cooldown:

Degree 10 20 40 60 80 100
Cooldown (% of base) 82.5% 76.5% 69.3% 64.8% 61.4% 58.7%

Boss damage steps every 20 Degrees. Elite bosses take double.

Degree 1–19 20–39 40–59 60–79 80–99 100
Normal boss ×1 ×1.25 ×1.5 ×1.75 ×2 ×2.25
Elite boss ×2 ×2.5 ×3 ×3.5 ×4 ×4.5

Aim for exactly 20, 40, 60, 80 or 100. Degree 39 is barely better than 20 against bosses.

Related extras: - Paragon of Power MK (Support, 14 points, 1,000 MM): a Paragon attacks faster (cooldown 83.33%) while no tier 5 of the same tower is on the map; 80.83% with Veteran Monkey Training. - Paragon Boost Perk (v56+): +25 Degrees to your first Paragon of the game. Perks are off in CHIMPS and competitive modes. - Totems are more cost-efficient than cash for every Paragon except Apex Plasma Master and Crucible. For those two, cash is cheaper per point under about $260k on Medium. - Turn Big Bloon Sabotage MK off when farming Degree in boss events. Blimps with less HP give less damage power. - Console versions were still on pre-57 Paragon rules when this data was collected (cash cap 60k, damage cap 90k, upgrade tiers counted).

Which to aim for first

# Paragon Why
1 Apex Plasma Master (Dart) Cheapest full build (≈$245k Medium, ≈$208k Easy) and lowest XP (622,300). Dart XP comes naturally. Learn the mechanics on a walled map
2 Crucible of Steel and Flame (Tack) ≈$293k, 500k unlock XP. Ability-driven
3 Herald of Everfrost (Ice) ≈$397k, 750k XP. The best cheap support Paragon for bosses and Freeplay
4 Glaive Dominus (Boomerang) ≈$506k, 500k XP. Reliable boss damage on any map
5 Ascended Shadow (Ninja) 500k XP. The Freeplay must-have
Later Goliath Doomship, Navarch of the Seas, Master Builder The strongest all-rounder, plus two support picks. Third-party rankings put Goliath first (unverified, mostly pre-v57)

Easy difficulty cuts the Paragon's price by about 15%, so a first Apex on an Easy or Medium game is fine.
