CHIMPS

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05

The exact CHIMPS rules, how to prepare, the cash you'll have at each danger round, and the best maps for a first win.

On this page

  1. The rules
  2. Cash checkpoints
  3. How to prepare
  4. Best maps for a first CHIMPS

CHIMPS is Hard prices (×1.08), 1 life, rounds 6–100, $650 to start, and no farms, Monkey Knowledge, Powers or selling. You get about $178.7k over the whole game. Every dollar has to sit in a tower that still works on round 100.

The rules

CHIMPS stands for no Continues, Hearts lost, Income, Monkey Knowledge, Powers or Selling. It unlocks on a map after you beat Impoppable there.

Rule Detail
Prices Hard (×1.08). Impoppable is ×1.2
Rounds 6–100, standard round set
Lives 1, and you can never gain more. Odyssey uses its own life pool
Starting cash $650. More Cash MK does not apply
Continues Off. Replaced by Retry Last Round
Income Only starting cash, pop cash and end-of-round cash. Banana Farms are disabled even if a challenge allows them. Double Cash is off
Traps Bloon Trap, XXXL Trap and Obyn's Wall of Trees pay only what the bloons would have paid
Map gimmicks Cash-paying gimmicks still work, such as the Three Mines 'Round crystals
Monkey Knowledge None of it applies
Powers Off, including Insta Monkeys. Hero Boosters, Fast Track and Perks are also off
Selling Not allowed. An indirect sale (a Support Chinook moving an Arctic Wind, for example) returns $0
Sell exceptions Champion Sentries, Paragon Sentries, Geraldo's level 18+ Creepy Idol and the Special Poperations Marine can still be sold
Sacrifices Adora's Blood Sacrifice and Sun Temple sacrifices don't count as selling
Rubber to Gold Pays nothing since 57.1
Lead to Gold Conflicting reports; plan as if it pays nothing (unverified)

Medals

  • Red CHIMPS: beat the map on CHIMPS at all, even using Retry Last Round.
  • Black CHIMPS: beat it in one session, without exiting the game and without Retry Last Round. You can come back later just for black.
  • In Co-op, a player who quits and rejoins gets only red. The others can still get black.
  • The black medal plus every other medal on the map gives the black border.

Retry Last Round makes red feel easier than Impoppable. Black is the real test.

Rules of thumb: - About 68% of the game's money arrives from R63 onward. The late game is where your cash is. - Pop cash drops to 10% at R86. Buy an expensive win condition in the early 80s, before R85. - A $100k (CHIMPS price) win condition bought before R85 leaves only about $19k for everything else (calc). - A 3-2-0 Sun Avatar costs $32,830 in CHIMPS. Entering R50 you have $34,559, which leaves $1,729 for everything else. Plan tier 5s for R60–80. - One sourced no-micro Monkey Meadow win finishes with about $91k unspent, so efficient routes use roughly half the available cash.

How to prepare

  1. Beat Impoppable on the map first. You need it to unlock CHIMPS anyway, and it teaches the map.
  2. Write the plan by round. Use the Danger Rounds list and the cash table above. The Planner prices each purchase at CHIMPS prices and places it in the first round you can afford it.
  3. Test it in Sandbox. Send R40, R63, R95, R98, R99 and R100 against your build.
  4. Go for red first. Use Retry Last Round freely to learn the weak rounds.
  5. Come back for black in one clean session.

Phase plan

R6–40 - An opener: 3× 0-0-0 Dart Monkey (one on Strong), or Dart + Sniper, Dart + Sub on water, or a Ninja 1-0-0 into 1-0-1. - Hero by about R15. - Camo by R24, lead by R28. - A MOAB answer for R40: Destroyer (Buccaneer 3-1-0), MOAB Mauler (Bomb x-3-x), Airburst Darts Sub with Berserker Brew into Triple Guns, Ezili at level 6, or Corvus.

R41–80 - A mid-game carry: Bloon Area Denial System (Dartling 0-0-4), Artillery Battery, Comanche Defense or Plasma Accelerator. - A win condition by R80: Carrier Flagship, Glaive Lord, Spiked Mines or Lord of the Abyss.

R81–100 - Supports: Jungle Drums (always), Giant Condor, Berserker Brew or Stronger Stimulant, Relentless Glue into Super Glue, MOAB Press 2-0-4, Bloon Sabotage, Glue Strike 1-4-0. - Situational: Monkey Intelligence Bureau, Embrittlement or Glue Storm, Overclock, Shattering Shells. - A second win condition is fine if both together cost under $100k. - Keep Spike Storm or First Strike Capability for R100.

Avoid in CHIMPS: Rubber to Gold, Bloon Master Alchemist, Benjamin, Merchantman / Trade Empire and Monkey City. They are income or economy tools that do nothing here.

Example route: Monkey Meadow, no micro

A community route with Gwendolin, no abilities, about $91k left over.

Round Buy
6 Tack Shooter 1-0-1
8 Tack 1-0-2, then 2-0-2
13 Gwendolin next to the Tack
19 Spike Factory 0-0-2 (end of round)
34–35 Spike Factory 0-0-4 Deadly Spikes, then 0-2-4
39 Tack 2-0-4 Overdrive (the source also says 0-2-4; unverified which)
41 Alchemist 3-0-0 Berserker Brew on the spikes

78 Spike Factory 0-2-5 Perma-Spike, and a second Alchemist to 4-0-0

The Tack Zone is the carry, buffed by Primary Training and Gwendolin. Perma-Spike catches leaks.

Best maps for a first CHIMPS

Long, single-lane maps with lots of track overlap.

Tier Maps
Beginner Logs (the usual first pick), Resort, Candy Falls, Spa Pits, Three Mines 'Round, Cubism, Four Circles, Monkey Meadow, Winter Park, Town Center, Park Path
Intermediate Sulfur Springs, KartsNDarts, Cracked, Streambed
Advanced Off The Coast, Cornfield
Expert Infernal, then Dark Castle

Leave Bloody Puddles, Ravine, Quad, #Ouch, Dark Dungeons, Muddy Puddles, X Factor and Geared for later. Erosion is brutal in CHIMPS: its collapsing land sells your towers for $0.
