Getting Started

v57.4 · checked 2026-11-05

A new player's route through BTD6 57.4, from first maps to first CHIMPS, with what to unlock and buy first.

On this page

  1. The route
  2. Medal order on each map
  3. Map tiers

  5. What not to buy

Win Easy on many different maps first. New map wins unlock the higher map tiers, and every first-time medal pays far more Monkey Money (MM) than a repeat.

The route

Step What to do Why
1 Finish the tutorial, then win Easy on 5 different Beginner maps Unlocks Intermediate maps
2 Keep winning on new maps: 12 for Advanced, 20 for Expert Repeat wins on the same map don't count
3 On each map you like, clear Easy, Primary Only and Deflation, then Medium, Military Only, Reverse and Apopalypse Fast medals, each with its own first-time MM payout
4 Play Hard Standard (rounds 3–80) with a few towers you want to level 2–6× the XP of an Easy or Medium game
5 Take the cheap Monkey Knowledge (MK) from level 30 Mo' Monkey Money and Monkey Education first
6 Unlock Expert and take Easy and Medium on Infernal and Dark Castle The two easiest Expert maps pay ×4 MM and ×1.3 XP
7 Try Magic Monkeys Only and Double HP MOABs Only a little harder than Hard Standard
8 First Impoppable, then CHIMPS, on a long easy map Logs, Resort, Candy Falls, Spa Pits
9 Black-border the easy maps, then work up the tiers Leave the True Experts, X Factor and Geared for last
10 Paragons, Elite bosses and Legends Once Hard feels comfortable

Good first Beginner maps: Logs, Resort, Monkey Meadow, Four Circles, Candy Falls.

Maps to leave until you're experienced: Bloody Puddles, Ravine, Quad, #Ouch, Dark Dungeons, Muddy Puddles, X Factor and Geared. Their early games need precise placement.

Medal order on each map

Each mode unlocks the next one on the same map.

Difficulty Unlock chain Border when done
Easy Standard → Primary Only → Deflation Bronze
Medium Standard → Military Only → Apopalypse; Standard → Reverse Silver (with all Easy medals)
Hard Standard → Magic Monkeys Only → Double HP MOABs → Half Cash
Hard Standard → Alternate Bloons Rounds → Impoppable → CHIMPS Gold (Hard, Impoppable and CHIMPS medals)
All Every medal including the black CHIMPS medal Black

Rules for each mode are on the Modes page.

Map tiers

Tier Unlock XP MM
Beginner Finish the tutorial ×1.0 ×1
Intermediate Win on 5 different maps ×1.1 ×2
Advanced Win on 12 different maps ×1.2 ×3
Expert Win on 20 different maps ×1.3 ×4

The tier sets rewards. It does not track real difficulty well: Geared is rated Advanced but plays harder than several Expert maps.

What to unlock first

Monkey Knowledge opens at player level 30. Take these first:

  1. Mo' Monkey Money (Magic tree, through Lingering Magic and Speedy Brewing, 0 points). +10% MM on wins and Daily Challenges.
  2. Monkey Education (Support tree, needs 3 points spent there). +8% tower and hero XP.
  3. Elite Military Training (Military tree, 0 points). +1,000 XP once to every Military tower and +5% Military XP.

The full order is on the Monkey Knowledge page.

Heroes. Quincy is free. Gwendolin, Striker Jones and Obyn Greenfoot unlock with player level. Common beginner picks:

Hero Cost to unlock Why
Obyn Greenfoot Free (level unlock) Strong with Magic towers
Benjamin 3,000 MM, level 15 Makes cash and lives each round. Useless in CHIMPS
Sauda 5,000 MM, level 25 Easy early game
Geraldo 7,000 MM, level 35 Shop items for many jobs, including Paragon Power Totems
Gwendolin Free (level unlock) Buffs fire towers and gives lead popping

What not to buy

  • Powers. Paragons ignore them and CHIMPS bans them. Use the free ones you get.
  • Continues and Hero Boosters.
  • Race retries unless you're close to a better bracket.

Habits that pay

  • Clear the Daily Challenge every day.
  • Each week: one free Race completion, the Easy Odyssey, Boss Event Normal tiers 1–2, and any Collection Event bonus maps.
  • Join a casual team for Contested Territory and Boss Rush.
  • Have camo detection by round 24, lead popping by round 28 and a MOAB answer before round 40. The Danger Rounds page lists the rest.
  • Test a CHIMPS plan in Sandbox before you play it for real.

Common beginner mistakes

  1. Spamming 0-0-0 towers. One well-placed 2-0-4 beats ten bare towers.
  2. Bomb-only or ice-only defences. Bombs can't pop Black or Zebra, and ice can't touch White or Zebra.
  3. Leaning on Magic towers into Purple rounds (25, 45, 95).
  4. Building the MOAB answer on round 39.
  5. Anti-MOAB towers left on First targeting.
  6. Firing abilities on easy rounds, so they're on cooldown for 63, 98 or 99.
  7. Farming after round 60, or greedily right before round 40.
  8. Forgetting that selling returns only 70%.
