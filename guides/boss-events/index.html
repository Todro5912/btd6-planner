Boss Events

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05

How weekly Boss Bloon Events work, plus each of the seven bosses' mechanics and the towers that counter them.

On this page

  1. How they work
  2. The seven bosses
  3. Quick counter table
  4. Related modes
  5. Useful Monkey Knowledge

You don't need to rank. Clear as many Normal tiers as you can for MM, Instas and Powers. Tiers 1–2 are reachable without Paragons; Tiers 3–5 and Elite runs are where Paragons matter.

How they work

Rule Detail
Schedule Weekly, Saturday 02:00 to Thursday 03:00 UTC
Setup One boss on one map, often with challenge rules. Usually Medium, starting at R1
Boss arrivals Tier 1 on R40, then R60, R80, R100, Tier 5 on R120
Losing If a tier is still alive when the next would spawn, or the boss reaches the exit, the run ends whatever your lives. R140 counts as failure
Normal / Elite Normal: 200 lives. Elite: 100 lives and tougher stats. Beat Normal Tier 5 solo before you can try Elite
Rewards MM, Instas and Powers per tier, plus a badge for all five tiers (Normal and Elite separately)
Ranked Powers, Double Cash and Fast Track off. Ranked by Fastest Time, Least Cash or Least Tiers
Checkpoints Every 20 rounds; loading one costs 100 MM, plus 100 more per checkpoint
Co-op Boss HP +20% per extra player

Rules for every boss: - Paragons deal extra boss damage: +25% per 20 Degrees, and double against Elite. - Bosses ignore percentage damage (Ezili's MOAB Hex, Grand Saboteur, Big Bloon Sabotage). - Like BADs, they ignore most slows, stuns, knockback and instant kills.

The seven bosses

Bloonarius, "The Inflator"
Mechanic Spawns slime-covered bloons along the whole track for every 1% of health it loses. At each skull it releases a huge wave in front of itself. Spawned bloons pay no cash
Counter Split the defence: boss damage (Elite Defender, Overdrive or The Tack Zone with Embrittlement, Avatar of Wrath with Poplust) plus high-pierce cleanup (Glaive Lord, The Bloon Solver, Spirit of the Forest). Master Builder is the best Paragon here

Lych, "The Gravelord"
Mechanic Heals itself (Regrow). Every 12s it steals temporary buffs from towers within 100 units (Brew, Overclock, Call to Arms; not Hero or Power buffs). Summons undead MOABs. At each skull it turns ethereal and spawns Lych-Souls you must kill. Cuts sell value to 35% while on screen. Reworked in v54
Counter Strip its regrow cheaply with Heart of Oak or Cleansing Foam. Keep buffed towers out of its range. Spread damage along the track. Root of all Nature is strong

Vortex, "Deadly Master of Air"
Mechanic Very fast. Gives all bloons 2.5× speed until they touch it. Its storm pulse deletes nearby projectiles. At each skull it shields, stuns nearby towers and gets knocked back
Counter Long or global range that stays out of stun range: Elite Defender, M.A.D, First Strike Capability, Monkey Aces, Heli Pilots, Carrier Flagship. Spread defences out. Stall it at the very start of the track

Dreadbloon, "Armored Behemoth"
Mechanic Lead properties and flat damage reduction. A ceramic armour shell regrows at every skull. Spawns Dreadrock bloons. Immune to one tower category at a time, cycling Primary, Military, Magic, Support (Elite cycles twice)
Counter Damage from at least two categories. Lead-busting Military damage (Pop and Awe, Plasma Accelerator) and Primary Paragons are popular

Phayze, "The Reality Warper"
Mechanic A Camo boss. Radar Jam (every 24s) turns off camo-granting buffs for 12s and drags bloons forward. A shield speeds it up. At each skull it warps ahead and moves where bloons spawn. A slow aura weakens towers more with each skull
Counter Towers with built-in camo detection, spread across the track. Ascended Shadow is strong here

Blastapopoulos, "Demon of the Core"
Mechanic Purple properties (immune to energy, fire and plasma). Clears damage-over-time every 2s. Hitting it fills a heat meter; when full it stuns all towers and lengthens ability cooldowns. Fireballs debuff towers and rocks shield it
Counter Cold attacks cool it: Icicles, Icicle Impale, Silas, Herald of Everfrost. Lean on base attacks more than abilities

Diamondback, "The Village Devourer"
Mechanic A segmented snake. The head deletes any projectile that pierces into it, and body segments cost projectiles 2 pierce. Breaking its fortified Rattle Tail stuns it for 12s and doubles all damage it takes. Scatters Diamond Bloons. From Frontier Legends (v52), in the base game since v54
Counter High-pierce attacks from the side or behind, and maps where it coils up. Master Bomber can stick bombs on several segments at once

Related modes

  • Boss Challenge: fight any boss whenever you like.
  • Boss Rush: a team event every other Wednesday 22:00 to Monday 22:00 UTC. Your team works through five islands of bosses with random relics and a shrinking tower roster. 4 tickets per player, refreshing every 6 hours. v57 lowered the early island targets (Island 1: 850 → 250 points). Apex Plasma Master is valued here for its low cost.

Useful Monkey Knowledge

  • Paragon of Power: faster Paragon attacks with no same-type tier 5 on the map. Almost essential.
  • Big Bloon Sabotage: turn it off when farming Paragon Degree, because blimps with less HP give less damage power.
