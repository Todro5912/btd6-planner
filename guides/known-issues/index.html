Settled recently

  • CHIMPS uses Hard prices (×1.08). Deflation uses Easy prices (×0.85).
  • Snipers need line of sight. Infernal has no LoS blockers (BTD6 Index), with 19.3 RBS on each lane.
  • Rubber to Gold pays nothing in CHIMPS since 57.1.
  • Desperado is a Primary tower.
  • Freeplay pop cash is 8% for R101–120 and 5% for R121–140 (v53).
  • Ship Capture is an Advanced map.
  • Lost Crevasse has 2 entrances and 2 exits feeding 3 lanes. Skates' three houses are one $500 purchase. Bloody Puddles has a $1,000 jeep and a $500 jeep-and-rocks.
