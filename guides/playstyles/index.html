Playstyles

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05

Eight ways to spend your cash, from safe to farm-heavy to CHIMPS-tight, with template builds and the modes each one suits.

On this page

  1. The eight archetypes
  2. Which playstyle for which mode
  3. Can a beginner afford it? (calc)
  4. For tower XP

Pick a playstyle by mode. Eco wins Hard and Impoppable. CHIMPS-tight is the only legal option in CHIMPS. Rush-to-tier-5 is what Deflation already is. The templates below are starting points; adjust them to the map.

The verdicts on this page are predictions from the cash numbers. None was play-tested.

The eight archetypes

1. Safe / defensive
Best for Easy, Medium, a first clear of a new map, Impoppable
Cash curve Spend 85–100% of income on defence. Your bank stays near $0
Template Opener → hero by R15 → camo by R24, lead by R28 → MOAB answer by R36 → one Village 2-2-0 → one tier-4 carry. At most one 2-0-0 farm before R40
Risks Slow money. You reach R63 and R80 with a thin bank and no tier 5. Over-building early wastes the cheapest cash in the game

2. Eco / farm-heavy
Best for Medium, Hard, ABR, Freeplay
Cash curve Below "safe" until about R35–45, then pulls ahead fast (a BRF pays $1,875 a round, Banana Central $7,500)
Template 2-0-0 farms from R15–30 while 3 Darts and a hero hold. A 2-3-0 Bank or 0-2-3 Marketplaces by R30–40. BRF 4-2-0s from R45–55. Banana Central 5-2-0 plus Monkey City by about R60. Sell farms into tier 5s
Risks The R40 MOAB, R45 camo purples and R63 ceramic rush punish greed. A farm bought after about R60–65 on Hard won't pay back by R80

3. Rush to tier 5
Best for Medium, Hard, Boss Events
Cash curve Saw-tooth: the bank climbs to the tier-5 price, drops to $0, repeats
Template A minimal cheap defence (Darts, hero, one tier 3) while you save, then one carry tier 5 early: Sun Avatar into Sun Temple, Glaive Lord, Elite Defender ($24.6k), Crossbow Master ($24.9k) or MOAB Eliminator ($32.4k)
Risks Everything else is under-built while you save. Camo, lead and purple gaps. One point of failure

4. Mono-tower
Best for Primary Only, Military Only, Magic Monkeys Only, tower XP grinding, Easy and Medium
Cash curve Same as safe, slightly worse value, because you can't buy the cheapest answer to each threat
Template A tower with an all-rounder tier 5 and crosspaths that patch its gaps: Dart Monkey, Ninja Monkey, Super Monkey, Sniper Monkey
Risks Every tower type has a hole: sharp vs Lead, magic vs Purple, bombs vs Black and Zebra. The crosspaths must cover it

5. Low-cost (LCC style)
Best for Black CHIMPS practice, record-style challenges
Cash curve Spent value stays far below earned cash. The bank grows all game
Template Buy only what the next danger round needs. Prefer cheap tier 3–4 towers with buffs (Jungle Drums, Berserker Brew, Overclock). Save the rest
Risks Zero margin. One misjudged round ends the run. Needs round knowledge

6. CHIMPS-tight
Best for CHIMPS, Impoppable
Cash curve Fixed: about $178.7k in total. About 68% of it arrives from R63 onward
Template Opener → hero by R15 → camo R24, lead R28 → MOAB answer R40 → mid carry R41–60 → win condition before R85 → supports → R100 burst
Risks No selling, no farms, 1 life. Mistakes are permanent. Every dollar must sit in a tower that keeps working to R100

7. Freeplay endurance
Best for After R100, Insta rewards at R200 and R300, Paragon fuel
Cash curve Almost all tower income. Pop cash is 8% at R101–120, 5% at R121–140 and 2% from R141
Template Farm hard before R100 (BRF spam, Banana Central, Monkeyopolis, Overclocks on farms), then convert into Paragons, Ultraboost, Homeland Defense and Permanent Brew. Big Bloon Sabotage MK on
Risks MOAB-class HP ramps +15% per round from about R125 and +35% from R151. BADs can't be slowed or stunned

Best Freeplay maps: Logs (fits 38 Banana Farms), then Resort. Midnight Mansion is the worst: no room for farms.

8. Boss event
Best for Boss Events, Normal and Elite
Cash curve Like eco until the boss arrives, then all-in on single-target damage
Template Strong single-target damage (Elite Defender, Cripple MOAB, Dark Champion, Paragons), funded by farms or Benjamin, boosted by Overclock and Ultraboost
Risks Tier HP walls. Elite needs much more damage. Reaching R140 counts as failure

See Boss Events for each boss.

Can a beginner afford it? (calc)

A sample "robust beginner" defence (3 Darts, Quincy, Ninja, Sniper 1-0-0, Druid of the Jungle, MOAB Mauler, Radar Village, Bloonjitsu, MOAB Assassin, Plasma Accelerator, Jungle Drums, Maim MOAB, MIB, MOAB Eliminator, Sun Avatar) checked against each mode's cash:

Mode Tightest point Share of cash used
Easy R40 38%
Medium R50 46%
Hard R63 71%
ABR R63 69%
Impoppable R98 83%
CHIMPS R98 75%
Half Cash R63 141%: unaffordable without banks, Benjamin and cheaper carries

Under 60% is comfortable, 60–85% tight. Half Cash is the only mode where this template fails. Each income dollar there buys 46% of what it buys on Medium (0.5 ÷ 1.08).

For tower XP

XP is split by cash invested. Mono-tower or rush to tier 5 puts the most money, and so the most XP, into the tower you're levelling. Use eco to fund it outside CHIMPS. Deflation's fixed $20,000 over 30 rounds is the best XP per minute. See XP Farming.
