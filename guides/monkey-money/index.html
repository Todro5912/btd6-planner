Monkey Money

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05

Where Monkey Money comes from, the fastest way to farm it, and what to spend it on first.

On this page

  1. Win rewards
  2. Other sources
  3. The farming plan
  4. What to spend it on first
  5. Trophies are separate

First-time medals are the biggest MM source by far. An Expert first win pays 300 to 1,200 MM, while a repeat pays 60 to 240. Collect every new medal on the highest map tier you can win, and take Mo' Monkey Money first.

Win rewards

MM scales with map tier: ×1, ×2, ×3 and ×4 for Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced and Expert.

  • The Expert row and Advanced Easy are confirmed from map pages. The other Advanced cells are ×3 of Beginner (calc).
  • Sub-modes (Primary Only, Deflation, Military Only, Apopalypse, Reverse, Magic Monkeys Only, Double HP MOABs, Half Cash, ABR, CHIMPS) each have their own medal. They are assumed to pay their parent difficulty's row, with a first-time bonus per new medal (unverified). CHIMPS is assumed to pay the Impoppable row.
  • Mo' Monkey Money adds 10%, rounded down.
  • BTD6+ and Netflix versions pay about 1.5×.

The farming plan

  1. Take Mo' Monkey Money. Magic tree: Lingering Magic → Speedy Brewing → Mo' Monkey Money, all 0 points. It pays for the rest of your MK.
  2. Collect first-time medals, highest tier first. Every map with medals has 14 mode medals, each with its own first payout. Easy and Medium sub-modes on Infernal and Dark Castle are fast and pay ×4.
  3. Do the daily and weekly chores. Daily chest, Daily Challenge, one free Race completion, the Easy Odyssey, Boss Event Normal tiers 1–2.
  4. Stack XP onto medal runs. Expert medals also pay ×1.3 tower XP. Use the tower you're levelling.
  5. Skip Golden Bloons and Continues. Pay for Race re-entries (100 MM, or 90 with Grand Prix Spree) only when a better bracket is close.

No source ranks specific maps by MM per hour, so any "single best MM map" claim is unverified. Once the medals are gone, repeat Impoppable wins on Expert (240 MM) are the best steady income.

What to spend it on first

  1. Monkey Knowledge. Tier 4 nodes cost 250 MM, tier 5 cost 500 MM, tier 6+ cost 1,000 MM. A respec costs 2,000 MM and keeps the MM you already paid unlocked. See Monkey Knowledge.
  2. A strong hero.

  1. Map Editor, from level 50, if you want it.
  2. Powers, sparingly. Paragons ignore them and CHIMPS bans them.
  3. Leave for later: Continues, Hero Boosters and cosmetics.

The Insta Store (v57) sells specific Insta Monkeys for MM. It rotates every two weeks with the Freeplay Challenge tab.

Trophies are separate

Trophies come from Races, Odysseys, Boss Events, CT and some achievements. They buy cosmetics in the Trophy Store and can't be turned into MM. Team Trophies from CT and Boss Rush buy team flair and CT relics.
