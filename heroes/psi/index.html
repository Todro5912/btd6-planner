About this data

Sources

  • Bloons Wiki: stats, prices, XP and map facts (CC BY-SA)
  • BTD6 Fandom wiki: second source used to cross-check prices and XP
  • BTD6 Index: lane lengths, water and line-of-sight flags, least-cost CHIMPS and solo-CHIMPS (2MP) records
  • Community strategy guides: crosspath picks, tier 5 rankings, roles and beginner tiers: opinions, not game data

How the numbers were made

  • Prices on Easy, Hard and Impoppable are worked out from the Medium price (x0.85, x1.08, x1.2), rounded to the nearest $5 per purchase. An Easy price can be $5 off where the game rounds a half down.
  • "Recommended on" lists the maps whose strategy on this site names this hero.
