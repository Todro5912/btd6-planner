Rounds 1–140

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05

The table below lists rounds 1–140 for the standard set and for Alternate Bloons Rounds.

Column Meaning
Contents The bloons in the round
RBE Total single hits to pop everything
Cash Pop cash plus the end-of-round bonus ($100 + round)
Cumulative Total cash earned from R1, without starting cash. Hard starts at R3 and Impoppable / CHIMPS at R6, so subtract the skipped rounds
XP Base XP for the round, before the map multiplier
  • Pop cash is 100% to R50, then 50% (R51–60), 20% (R61–85), 10% (R86–100), 8% (R101–120) and 5% (R121–140).
  • Half Cash halves every cash figure. Deflation pays no cash at all.
  • XP is 20r + 20 for R1–20, 40(r − 20) + 420 for R21–50 and 90(r − 50) + 1,620 from R51. Freeplay rounds after a win pay 30% (to R100) or 10% (after).
  • The danger rating is an opinion based on community round lists.

The rounds that end most runs, and what each needs, are on the Danger Rounds page.

Sources: Bloons Wiki, Cyber Quincy, Ninja Kiwi patch notes.
