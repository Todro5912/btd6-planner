What this is

  • Fast answers for when you're in a game: what pops camo, lead, purple and MOABs, and the cheapest fix on your difficulty.
  • A page for every tower, hero, map and round 1–100.
  • Tools for between games: the simulator, XP calculator and defence check.
  • Unofficial. Not made, checked or endorsed by Ninja Kiwi. No game art is used.

How it's made

  • {{ site.author }} is a BTD6 player and the developer. The site is built with AI assistance: research, code and copy are drafted with an AI model, then checked and edited by {{ site.author }}.
  • Numbers come from the sources below for game version {{ meta.version }}.
