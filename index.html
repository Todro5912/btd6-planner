Prices for

Tap what's leaking. You get the rule and the cheapest fixes.

  • Camo Camo needs detection. Towers without it won't shoot them. from $400
  • Lead Lead needs non-sharp damage. Darts can't. from $375
  • Purple Purple ignores Energy, Fire and Plasma. from $200
  • Black / Zebra Black ignores explosions. Zebra ignores explosions and ice. from $200
  • White / Frozen White ignores ice. Frozen Bloons can't be popped by sharp attacks. from $250
  • Fortified Fortified is just more HP. Every tower can pop it. No special upgrade needed
  • MOAB-class Almost every attack hurts a MOAB. You need damage, not a special key. from $950
  • DDT A DDT is camo + Lead + Black, and fast. One tower must handle all three. from $950
