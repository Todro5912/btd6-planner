Easy and Medium

Darts + Quincy in the middle, Dartling or Heli, Village, Bomb for Lead, Mauler for R40. Save the rocket for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Wiki: The Bloon Solver on the crater near the middle (covers four sections) + Carpet of Spikes; or BEZ 2-0-5 with camo, Stronger Stimulant, Lead support and Overclock. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: BADS/BEZ + Brew handle it; aim the Dartling at the start of the dense ceramic waves. R76: Grow Blocker is part of the 2-2-0/2-3-0 Village. R90/R95: MIB lets the buckshot hit DDTs; the BEZ shotgun handles the 500 camo purples. R98: BEZ + Stronger Stimulant; Eliminator on Strong. R100: MOAB Eliminator ability on the BAD after its outer layer cracks.

Placement and XP

Placement. The crater near the middle (Solver) and decent-range towers in the middle. Exploit the 40% slow zone on the grass for Spike Factories.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 45,950 tower XP here (Beginner x1.0) vs 59,735 on an Expert map (calc from the XP formula). Above-average Beginner. Lots of LoS blockers: so-so for Snipers.
