Three Mines 'Round

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Beginner 1 lane Easiness 5/5 water: little · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator

Removable Bananite crystals return cash (works in Deflation/CHIMPS). Among the easiest Beginner maps. Crystal rocks and the tent block LoS; crystal payouts work in CHIMPS, Deflation and Half Cash.

How to beat it

Key rounds
  1. R63have the Flagship plus Stronger Stimulant by R62; on Impoppable add a 2nd Destroyer if the Flagship is late.
  2. R76the 2-2-0 Village already has Grow Blocker, so regrow ceramics stay down.
  3. R90DDTs need camo + Lead + Black: Radar + MIB (2-3-0), or Brickell's Naval Tactics (water towers do Normal damage).
  4. R95Glaive Lord + MIB clean the purples; pop Naval Tactics.
  5. R98Flagship + Glaive; fire Spike Storm and Naval Tactics together.
  6. R100Mega Mines (Brickell) or Spike Storm as the BAD comes in; the Flagship planes finish it.

Easy and Medium

  • Mine the $435 crystal on R1.
  • Darts + Quincy near the intersections, Village, Bomb for Lead, Mauler/Destroyer for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Crystals: $435->$870, $3,000->$4,600, $10,000->$15,000, $25,000->$37,000, $50,000->$72,000, $100,000->$160,000 (corner, 20 rounds).
  • The cheapest is almost always worth it
  • Outside CHIMPS/Deflation/Half Cash the rest are worse than farms
  • Skip the $100k one except for CHIMPS challenges.

Follow the CHIMPS build, then the general Hard and Impoppable plan (farms, lives, prices).

CHIMPS

  • Known route (Quincy, Flagship 5-2-0, Prince of Darkness 0-2-5, Glaive Lord 5-0-2; no round numbers given).
  • Timings and crystal schedule calculated; PoD can replace the Spike Factory.
  • Custom route.
  • Spend by R40: $13,585 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250).
  • Total build $129,560 of $199,379 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$69,819 spare calculated.
  • Includes $20,470 of crystal payouts calculated.

Where to place

  • Near the points where the track intersects (better than the tempting centre, where most towers chase).
  • An AoE tower (e.g. a range-buffed Inferno Ring) can use the centre.

Removable: Bananite crystal ($435 Medium, first of 6): six crystals at $435 / $3,000 / $10,000 / $25,000 / $50,000 / $100,000 ($188,435 in total); each PAYS OUT after 10 rounds (20 for the $100k one): $870 / $4.6k / $15k / $37k / $72k / $160k, even in Deflation and CHIMPS
