Easy and Medium

Mine the $435 crystal on R1. Darts + Quincy near the intersections, Village, Bomb for Lead, Mauler/Destroyer for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Crystals: $435->$870, $3,000->$4,600, $10,000->$15,000, $25,000->$37,000, $50,000->$72,000, $100,000->$160,000 (corner, 20 rounds). The cheapest is almost always worth it; outside CHIMPS/Deflation/Half Cash the rest are worse than farms; skip the $100k one except for CHIMPS challenges. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: have the Flagship plus Stronger Stimulant by R62; on Impoppable add a 2nd Destroyer if the Flagship is late. R76: the 2-2-0 Village already has Grow Blocker, so regrow ceramics stay down. R90: DDTs need camo + Lead + Black: Radar + MIB (2-3-0), or Brickell's Naval Tactics (water towers do Normal damage). R95: Glaive Lord + MIB clean the purples; pop Naval Tactics. R98: Flagship + Glaive; fire Spike Storm and Naval Tactics together. R100: Mega Mines (Brickell) or Spike Storm as the BAD comes in; the Flagship planes finish it.

CHIMPS

Known route (Quincy, Flagship 5-2-0, Prince of Darkness 0-2-5, Glaive Lord 5-0-2; no round numbers given). Timings and crystal schedule calculated; PoD can replace the Spike Factory. Custom route. Spend by R40: $13,585 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250). Total build $129,560 of $199,379 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$69,819 spare calculated. Placement and XP

Placement. Near the points where the track intersects (better than the tempting centre, where most towers chase). An AoE tower (e.g. a range-buffed Inferno Ring) can use the centre.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 45,950 tower XP here (Beginner x1.0) vs 59,735 on an Expert map calculated. Among the easiest Beginner maps, and the crystals still pay in Deflation ($435 crystal = +$435 profit), so it's a strong Beginner Deflation pick.
