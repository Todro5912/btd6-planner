Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Darts on each side early, Boomerangs on the peak, Village for camo, Bomb for Lead, MOAB Press for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Wiki: win conditions on the top 2 peak spots. MOAB Domination with Primary Training, Radar and Stronger Stimulant "trivializes the entire late-game", including the R100 BAD; Glaive Lord catches leaks. In CHIMPS only put a tower on the peak early if it will become the win condition, and don't crowd the lower layer next to the top 2 spots (it can block placement). Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: M.O.A.R Glaives + Primary Training; MOAB Press bounces the R64 MOABs. R76: Grow Blocker is in the 3-2-0 Village. R90: Red Hot Rangs (x-x-2) pop Lead, Radar gives camo, rangs are sharp so Black is fine. R95: Glaive Lord + MOAB Domination. R98: MOAB Domination's press attack hits the BFBs/ZOMGs across most of the map. R100: wiki says MOAB Domination with Primary Training, Radar and Stronger Stimulant handles the BAD; Spike Storm as backup.

Placement and XP

Placement. Top 2 peak spots for MOAB Domination and Glaive Lord. Water towers in the edge ponds; helpers on lower layers away from the peak footprint.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 50,545 tower XP here (Intermediate x1.1) vs 59,735 on an Expert map (calc from the XP formula). Four entrances make it busy; so-so for farming. Snipers blocked by the temple tiers unless on top.
