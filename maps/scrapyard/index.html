Scrapyard

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Beginner 1 lane Easiness 4/5 water: none · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator

Longest map in the game; clickable crusher; junk pile blocks LoS; four elevated crates hold towers.

How to beat it

Key rounds
  1. R63BADS/BEZ + Brew handle it; aim the Dartling at the start of the dense ceramic waves.
  2. R76Grow Blocker is part of the 2-2-0/2-3-0 Village.
  3. R98BEZ + Stronger Stimulant; Eliminator on Strong.
  4. R100MOAB Eliminator ability on the BAD after its outer layer cracks.

Easy and Medium

  • Darts + Quincy on the top-left box, Village above it, Bomb for Lead, Mauler for R40.
  • Use the crusher on the first camo/lead waves.

Hard and Impoppable

  • BEZ on a box is the "easy carry" and most optimal pick; BADS saves up to it.
  • Fire the crusher on dense non-MOAB rounds (R63 ceramics) - glue first boosts it.
  • R90/R95: MIB lets the buckshot hit DDTs; the BEZ shotgun handles the 500 camo purples.

Follow the CHIMPS build, then the general Hard and Impoppable plan (farms, lives, prices).

CHIMPS

  • Core build: BEZ.
  • Spend by R40: $15,980 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250).
  • Total build $137,320 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$41,589 spare calculated.
