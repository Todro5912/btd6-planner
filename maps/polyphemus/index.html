Hard and Impoppable

Wiki: Carrier Flagship 5-2-0 in the eye (Destroyer helps the mid game and gives partial DDT popping); an Arctic Wind on its platform opens deeper eye spots for land towers (eye buffs apply on the Arctic Wind, not on Flagship platforms); Giant Condor drags blimps between lanes so most rounds are effectively single-lane. Eye reopening costs (calc from the wiki rule, R15..R95): $500 + $1,000 + ... + $4,500 = $22,500 over a CHIMPS game. Chapter 4 quoted the wiki as "$26,000 total up to round 100"; I couldn't reconcile the two. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: have the Flagship plus Stronger Stimulant by R62; on Impoppable add a 2nd Destroyer if the Flagship is late. R76: the 2-2-0 Village already has Grow Blocker, so regrow ceramics stay down. R90: DDTs need camo + Lead + Black: Radar + MIB (2-3-0), or Brickell's Naval Tactics (water towers do Normal damage). R95: Glaive Lord + MIB clean the purples; pop Naval Tactics. R98: Flagship + Glaive; fire Spike Storm and Naval Tactics together. R100: Mega Mines (Brickell) or Spike Storm as the BAD comes in; the Flagship planes finish it.

Map note: Build the Buccaneer, Alchemist and Village in the eye (15% off: the Destroyer->Flagship upgrades alone save about $6,050, calc). Reopen the eye at the start of R16, 26, 36 ... 96. Placement and XP

Placement. In the eye (discount, speed, camo). Land towers directly above and below the eye.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 50,545 tower XP here (Intermediate x1.1) vs 59,735 on an Expert map (calc from the XP formula). Average; the eye fees make Deflation (no income) awkward.
