Easy and Medium

Darts + Quincy in the centre, Boomerang 3-0-2, Village, Lead popper, MOAB Press/Mauler for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Wiki: MOAB Domination in the centre handles the late game; The Bloon Solver covers several sectors and pairs with it. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: M.O.A.R Glaives + Primary Training; MOAB Press bounces the R64 MOABs. R76: Grow Blocker is in the 3-2-0 Village. R90: Red Hot Rangs (x-x-2) pop Lead, Radar gives camo, rangs are sharp so Black is fine. R95: Glaive Lord + MOAB Domination. R98: MOAB Domination's press attack hits the BFBs/ZOMGs across most of the map. R100: wiki says MOAB Domination with Primary Training, Radar and Stronger Stimulant handles the BAD; Spike Storm as backup.
