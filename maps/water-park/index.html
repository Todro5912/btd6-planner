Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Boomerang on the pink slide (hits both lanes), Heli Pilot, Village, Bomb, MOAB Shove for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Heli Pilots "excel"; Comanche Commander + Glue Storm + Sharpening Stone; MOAB Domination on the pink slide reaches both lanes, with Primary Training, Radar and Stronger Stimulant. A common CHIMPS start is a Boomerang, 0-1-1 into 0-1-2. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: Comanche Defense + Glue Strike ability on wave 2. R76: Grow Blocker is in the 2-2-0 Village. R90: Glue Storm de-Leads DDTs, so no MIB needed; Radar for camo. R95: Comanche Commander + Glue Storm + Perma-Spike. R98: Glue Storm ability + Comanche; Geraldo's Genie Bottle if bought. R100: Comanche + Perma-Spike; MOAB Shove stalls blimps but not the BAD.

CHIMPS

Core build: Comanche. Spend by R40: $12,400 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250). Total build $133,765 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$45,144 spare calculated.

Placement and XP

Placement. The pink slide top-right of the 2nd loop (straight lines on both paths) for limited-range towers; pink platforms in the loops; lifeguard tower. Helis anywhere.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 50,545 tower XP here (Intermediate x1.1) vs 59,735 on an Expert map calculated. Hard: don't farm here.
