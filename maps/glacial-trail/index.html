Strategies confidence: high

Easy and Medium

Base Mortar early (bends), Dart line to Juggernaut (walls for bouncing), Silas + a few Ice Monkeys. Stagger placements so you never have everything frozen at once.

Hard and Impoppable

Perma-Spike 2-0-5, Ultra-Juggernaut, Icicle Impale (never freezes, stalls ZOMGs/DDTs every round). Non-CHIMPS: sell towers about to freeze if you need cash.

CHIMPS

Rules of thumb: place most late-game towers on rounds ending in 2 or 4; lead-poppers on rounds ending 2; camo towers on rounds ending 0; after R91 placement doesn't matter. Win conditions: Perma-Spike 2-0-5, Ultra-Juggernaut, Icicle Impale, Silas. Base Mortar ($650) can be bought on R6 because its price was cut in v50.

Placement and XP

Placement. Mortar at the bends; Juggernauts where balls bounce off cliffs; Perma-Spike near the exit; Ice Monkeys where Silas can reach.

XP farm. Poor Deflation farm: everything placed on R31 freezes on R40-41, 50-51 and 60. Stagger placements (R31/R33/R35) or use Ice (immune).
