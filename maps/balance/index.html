Strategies confidence: high

Easy and Medium

Sauda at the central loop + Darts; Tack 2-0-3, Village, Lead popper, Overdrive for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Wiki: Overdrive near the top of the central spot with Brew; Embrittlement near the centre; Artillery Battery targeting the loop for the mid game, Pop and Awe + Relentless Glue late, Spike Storm for the BAD. Don't spend central-loop spots on early towers you won't upgrade. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: Tack Zone (R60) + Sauda + Brew at the loop. R76: Embrittlement (+1 damage) and Grow Blocker in the Village. R90: Embrittlement strips Lead and camo off DDTs. R95: Tack Zone + Sauda; keep Embrittlement in range of the loop. R98: Relentless Glue stuns + Sauda's abilities. R100: Perma-Spike + Sauda + Tack Zone; add a Spike Storm or MOAB Eliminator if short on single-target.

Placement and XP

Placement. Around the central loop (most valuable). Early cheap towers near the entrances instead, so the loop is free for carries. Two small pools take water towers (Index: water yes; fandom).

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 50,545 tower XP here (Intermediate x1.1) vs 59,735 on an Expert map (calc from the XP formula). Easy and loop-heavy: good for Tack/Mortar XP. Hedges limit Snipers.
