Easy and Medium

Darts + hero by the start of the bloon lane, Heli Pilot, Village, Bomb, and a MOAB answer aimed at the MOAB lane for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Wiki: Prince of Darkness (zombies ignore LoS, long track gives time to spawn, good vs R95 DDTs) with Ezili/Corvus; Comanche Commander (ignores LoS, shove stops it chasing fast stream bloons) with Geraldo + Glue Storm. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: Comanche Defense + Glue Strike ability on wave 2. R76: Grow Blocker is in the 2-2-0 Village. R90: Glue Storm de-Leads DDTs (wiki), so no MIB needed; Radar for camo. R95: Comanche Commander + Glue Storm + Perma-Spike. R98: Glue Storm ability + Comanche; Geraldo's Genie Bottle if bought. R100: Comanche + Perma-Spike; MOAB Shove stalls blimps but not the BAD.

Placement and XP

Placement. Bloon-poppers along the long zig-zag; blimp damage near the MOAB lane (top-right path). Heli covers both.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 50,545 tower XP here (Intermediate x1.1) vs 59,735 on an Expert map (calc from the XP formula). Easy and long: good Intermediate farm for Heli/Wizard XP. Walls block Snipers.
