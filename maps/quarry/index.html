Quarry

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Intermediate 2 lanes Easiness 5/5 water: little · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator

Bloon lane + MOAB lane; elevation tiers block LoS; a dump truck moves off the MOAB lane after R39. One of the easiest Intermediate maps.

How to beat it

Key rounds
  1. R63Comanche Defense + Glue Strike ability on wave 2.
  2. R76Grow Blocker is in the 2-2-0 Village.
  3. R90Glue Storm de-Leads DDTs, so no MIB needed; Radar for camo.
  4. R95Comanche Commander + Glue Storm + Perma-Spike.
  5. R98Glue Storm ability + Comanche; Geraldo's Genie Bottle if bought.
  6. R100Comanche + Perma-Spike; MOAB Shove stalls blimps but not the BAD.

Easy and Medium

  • Darts + hero by the start of the bloon lane, Heli Pilot, Village, Bomb, and a MOAB answer aimed at the MOAB lane for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Prince of Darkness (zombies ignore LoS, long track gives time to spawn, good vs R95 DDTs) with Ezili/Corvus
  • Comanche Commander (ignores LoS, shove stops it chasing fast stream bloons) with Geraldo + Glue Storm.

Follow the CHIMPS build, then the general Hard and Impoppable plan (farms, lives, prices).
