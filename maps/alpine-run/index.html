Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Quincy + 2 Darts on the middle lane. Boomerang 3-0-2 (Glaive Ricochet) in the centre of the middle lane by R20, Village 0-2-0 for camo by R24, Bomb 0-2-0 for R28 Leads. Bomb 0-3-0 MOAB Mauler on Strong before R40. Medium: add two Banana Farms 2-3-0 around R15-30, then Boomerang 4-0-2 + Alchemist 3-0-0 for R50-60.

Hard and Impoppable

Same core as CHIMPS; put the farms in the dead corners and rush MOAB Domination. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: M.O.A.R Glaives + Primary Training; MOAB Press bounces the R64 MOABs. R76: Grow Blocker is in the 3-2-0 Village. R90: Red Hot Rangs (x-x-2) pop Lead, Radar gives camo, rangs are sharp so Black is fine. R95: Glaive Lord + MOAB Domination. R98: MOAB Domination's press attack hits the BFBs/ZOMGs across most of the map. R100: MOAB Domination with Primary Training, Radar and Stronger Stimulant handles the BAD; Spike Storm as backup.

CHIMPS

Core build: MOAB Domination + Glaive. Spend by R40: $14,975 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250). Total build $125,515 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$53,394 spare calculated.

Placement and XP

Placement. Centre of the middle lane, between the ski-lift lines, sees almost every straight. Put MOAB Domination "between the centre and the last bend" and a BEZ or Spike Factory near the exit as leak insurance.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 45,950 tower XP here (Beginner x1.0) vs 59,735 on an Expert map calculated. No LoS blockers, so Snipers see everything from anywhere: one of the better Beginner maps for Sniper Deflation. Good for Boomerang and Dartling XP too.
