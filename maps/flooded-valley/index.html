Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Two Subs, then Destroyer 3-2-0 for R40; Mermonkey in the water.

Hard and Impoppable

Carrier Flagship (solid mid-game, can save into the 80s), Absolute Zero (intersecting lanes keep refreezing), Silas.

CHIMPS

Wiki: Carrier Flagship (not affordable until the late 50s in CHIMPS), Absolute Zero, Silas/Brickell. A secondary CHIMPS guide (bloon.games) uses Subs -> Silas -> Mermonkey 4-0-2/5-0-2 + Destroyer -> Flagship -> Ice T5s; my cash check shows you can't afford both Super Brittle and Absolute Zero plus Flagship, so the plan keeps only Absolute Zero.

Placement and XP

Placement. All early towers in the right-side water. Land Primaries only on: bottom rightmost rock (Crossbow, Glaives, Big Iron), rightmost top/bottom rocks (Sharp Shooter); the rocks are the only land spots that see over the dam (wiki).

XP farm. Middle of the Expert Deflation table: water monos only (Destroyers/Subs/Mermonkey).
