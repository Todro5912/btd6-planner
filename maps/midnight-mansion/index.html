Strategies confidence: low

Easy and Medium

Tack Shooter 2-0-3 at the overlap, Dart 0-0-2 camo, Bomb for lead, Bomb 0-3-0 or Overdrive for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Overdrive -> Tack Zone at the overlap with a Primary Training village and Brew, Perma-Spike as leak tank. Farm is hard to fit, so lean on Benjamin on Impoppable.
