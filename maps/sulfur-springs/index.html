Easy and Medium

Darts + hero, Buccaneer in the central spring, Village, Destroyer. Almost anything works.

Hard and Impoppable

Wiki: Ultra-Juggernaut on a low rock (balls bounce off its walls; Apex Plasma Master also shines); five Destroyers with camo and Stronger Stimulant -> Permanent Brew (8x damage) with Brickell. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: have the Flagship plus Stronger Stimulant by R62; on Impoppable add a 2nd Destroyer if the Flagship is late. R76: the 2-2-0 Village already has Grow Blocker, so regrow ceramics stay down. R90: DDTs need camo + Lead + Black: Radar + MIB (2-3-0), or Brickell's Naval Tactics (water towers do Normal damage). R95: Glaive Lord + MIB clean the purples; pop Naval Tactics. R98: Flagship + Glaive; fire Spike Storm and Naval Tactics together. R100: Mega Mines (Brickell) or Spike Storm as the BAD comes in; the Flagship planes finish it.

Placement and XP

Placement. Central hot spring for Buccaneers. Juggernaut on one of the three low rocks.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 50,545 tower XP here (Intermediate x1.1) vs 59,735 on an Expert map (calc from the XP formula). The best Intermediate farm (x1.1 XP, easiest). Good for Dart (Juggernaut) and Buccaneer XP.
