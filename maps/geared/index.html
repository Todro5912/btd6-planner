Geared

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Advanced 1 lane Easiness 1/5 water: none · line of sight: clear Open in Simulator

Two gear platforms rotate every round, carrying your towers. Harder than several Expert maps, mostly because of the early game.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • 3-4 base Snipers (rotation barely matters to them), then a 2-0-2 Sniper for lead and 0-1-2 for camo, Heli Comanche Defense by R50.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Snipers carry until Heli/Ace become affordable.
  • Comanche Defense 0-2-4 -> Comanche Commander 0-2-5, or Spectre.
  • Key rounds: R6–20 (only Snipers work), R40 (Snipers + MOAB Shove Heli), R63 (Comanche Defense).

CHIMPS

  • Snipers for the early game, then global Heli (Comanche Defense/Commander or Apache) or Spectre as the win condition.
