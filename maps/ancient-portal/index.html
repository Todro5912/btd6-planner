Ancient Portal

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Intermediate 2 lanes Easiness 3/5 water: little · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator

One entrance spirals into a portal that sends bloons to one of two exits; you can switch the active exit for free.

How to beat it

Key rounds
  1. R63Artillery Battery/Pop and Awe on the loop; fire Pop and Awe on the 2nd and 3rd waves.
  2. R76Grow Blocker is in the 2-2-0 Village.
  3. R90Striker removes Black immunity for explosions; Radar for camo; MIB later.
  4. R95Pop and Awe + Perma-Spike.
  5. R98Pop and Awe ability + Relentless Glue stuns.
  6. R100Pop and Awe is weak vs the BAD: Perma-Spike stack + Spike Storm / First Strike if you add one.

Easy and Medium

  • Realign to the left (longer) path early.
  • Darts + Striker, Mortar for Lead/camo, Village, Artillery Battery or Mauler for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Pop and Awe on the bends (realign left for more track), BAD covered by First Strike or Spike Storm
  • Perma-Spike covers PnA's DDT weakness
  • Overdrive/Tack Zone near a bend with Geraldo, Embrittlement, Brew and Primary Training.

Follow the CHIMPS build, then the general Hard and Impoppable plan (farms, lives, prices).

Where to place

  • Hero on a pedestal (top near entrance, below the spiral, in the pool, or in the ruins).
  • PnA targeting the bends on both ends of the spiral.
  • Perma-Spike at the active exit.
  • LoS: the left (curved) path has blockers, the right path none.
