Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Realign to the left (longer) path early. Darts + Striker, Mortar for Lead/camo, Village, Artillery Battery or Mauler for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Wiki: Pop and Awe on the bends (realign left for more track), BAD covered by First Strike or Spike Storm; Perma-Spike covers PnA's DDT weakness; Overdrive/Tack Zone near a bend with Geraldo, Embrittlement, Brew and Primary Training. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: Artillery Battery/Pop and Awe on the loop; fire Pop and Awe on the 2nd and 3rd waves. R76: Grow Blocker is in the 2-2-0 Village. R90: Striker removes Black immunity for explosions; Radar for camo; MIB later. R95: Pop and Awe + Perma-Spike. R98: Pop and Awe ability + Relentless Glue stuns. R100: Pop and Awe is weak vs the BAD (wiki): Perma-Spike stack + Spike Storm / First Strike if you add one.

Placement and XP

Placement. Hero on a pedestal (top near entrance, below the spiral, in the pool, or in the ruins). PnA targeting the bends on both ends of the spiral. Perma-Spike at the active exit. LoS: the left (curved) path has blockers, the right path none (bloonswiki; Index LoS yes).

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 50,545 tower XP here (Intermediate x1.1) vs 59,735 on an Expert map (calc from the XP formula). Middle-of-the-road Intermediate; fine for Mortar/Tack XP. Snipers fine on the straight lane, blocked on the curved one.
