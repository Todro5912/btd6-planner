Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Dart + Sub in the pond, Ninja 2-0-1 for camo, Destroyer 3-2-0 for R40, Village 2-2-0.

Hard and Impoppable

Carrier Flagship 5-2-0 with Brickell, The Bloon Solver on the $750 lower-middle building to cover both lanes, Comanche Commander as an alternative. Heli is solid because the lanes are single and alternating.

CHIMPS

Carrier Flagship 5-2-0 + Brickell, with The Bloon Solver on the lower-middle $750 building (puddles chain-pop Super Ceramics). Alternative: Comanche Commander + Geraldo + Glue Storm.
