High Finance

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Advanced 2 lanes Easiness 3/5 water: little · line of sight: some Open in Simulator

Two alternating lanes; pond split by a bridge. Average. Clearing all 15 plots costs $12,550.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Dart + Sub in the pond, Ninja 2-0-1 for camo, Destroyer 3-2-0 for R40, Village 2-2-0.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Carrier Flagship 5-2-0 with Brickell, The Bloon Solver on the $750 lower-middle building to cover both lanes, Comanche Commander as an alternative.
  • Heli is solid because the lanes are single and alternating.

CHIMPS

  • Carrier Flagship 5-2-0 + Brickell, with The Bloon Solver on the lower-middle $750 building (puddles chain-pop Super Ceramics).
  • Alternative: Comanche Commander + Geraldo + Glue Storm.
