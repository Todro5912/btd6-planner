Sunset Gulch

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Advanced 2 lanes Easiness 3/5 water: none · line of sight: clear Plan this map Open in Simulator Check my defence

Bloon path + short MOAB path; cliffs and cacti. Middle of the tier.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Concentrate near the end of the map; left side supplementary early, right side later.
  • Dart 0-0-2 + Desperado + Bomb 0-3-0 aimed at the MOAB rail.

Hard and Impoppable

  • The Desert Phantom 2-0-5 (straight MOAB lane) or Golden Justice; Perma-Spike on Set Target at the lane intersection.

CHIMPS

  • The Desert Phantom 2-0-5 along the straight MOAB lane, Perma-Spike (Set Target at the intersection), The Bloon Solver for Super Ceramics.
  • Alternative (bloon.games): Obyn + Elite Defender 0-2-5 Sniper + Monarch of Storms Druid (5-x-x, formerly Superstorm).
