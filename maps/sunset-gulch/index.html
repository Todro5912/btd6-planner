Easy and Medium

Concentrate near the end of the map; left side supplementary early, right side later. Dart 0-0-2 + Desperado + Bomb 0-3-0 aimed at the MOAB rail.

Hard and Impoppable

The Desert Phantom 2-0-5 (straight MOAB lane) or Golden Justice; Perma-Spike on Set Target at the lane intersection.
