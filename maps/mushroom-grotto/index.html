Mushroom Grotto

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Advanced 2 lanes Easiness 2/5 water: none · line of sight: heavy Plan this map Open in Simulator

Darkness: towers cannot see into or be placed in the dark until you light mushroom patches. Hardest in the early game.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Mortar aimed at the lit centre crossing, Darts in the light, light the four $250 patches by R20, then normal play.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Mortar and Dartling fire without needing a visible target (darkness), but Dartling darts are still stopped by LoS blockers, so aim it down an open stretch.
  • Together they carry the dark early game.
  • Carpet of Spikes: with no mushrooms lit, its spikes always land in the middle, concentrating damage.

CHIMPS

  • Mortar/Dartling early and Carpet of Spikes (keep mushrooms unlit so spikes concentrate in the middle).
  • The late-game part of this route is unverified.
