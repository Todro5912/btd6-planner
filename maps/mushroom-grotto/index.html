Strategies confidence: low

Easy and Medium

Mortar aimed at the lit centre crossing, Darts in the light, light the four $250 patches by R20, then normal play.

Hard and Impoppable

Mortar and Dartling fire without needing a visible target (darkness), but Dartling darts are still stopped by LoS blockers, so aim it down an open stretch. Together they carry the dark early game. Carpet of Spikes: with no mushrooms lit, its spikes always land in the middle, concentrating damage.

CHIMPS

Mortar/Dartling early and Carpet of Spikes (keep mushrooms unlit so spikes concentrate in the middle). The late-game part of this route is unverified.
