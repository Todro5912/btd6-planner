Strategies confidence: high

Easy and Medium

Anything. Darts + Quincy, Buccaneer 2-2-0 in the pond, Village 0-2-0, Destroyer for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

A known CHIMPS route: Pirate Lord 2-5-0 + Brickell + Village 2-2-0 + Stronger Stimulant "with about $100,000 to spare". Destroyer 3-2-0 with Brew and camo carries the mid game. For freeplay, Logs fits the most Banana Farms (38). Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: have the Flagship plus Stronger Stimulant by R62; on Impoppable add a 2nd Destroyer if the Flagship is late. R76: the 2-2-0 Village already has Grow Blocker, so regrow ceramics stay down. R90: DDTs need camo + Lead + Black: Radar + MIB (2-3-0), or Brickell's Naval Tactics (water towers do Normal damage). R95: Glaive Lord + MIB clean the purples; pop Naval Tactics. R98: Flagship + Glaive; fire Spike Storm and Naval Tactics together. R100: Mega Mines (Brickell) or Spike Storm as the BAD comes in; the Flagship planes finish it.

CHIMPS

Known route: Pirate Lord 2-5-0 + Brickell + Village 2-2-0 + Stronger Stimulant, with about $100,000 to spare; this order leaves ~$99k calculated. BTD6 Index record: cheapest CHIMPS ever, $1,215 with only Corvus + a 0-0-0 Dart. Custom route. Spend by R40: $13,750 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250). Total build $79,625 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$99,284 spare calculated.

Placement and XP

Placement. The central pond: track surrounds it, so Destroyer/Pirate Lord guns fire on both sides. Land towers at the intersections in the middle.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 45,950 tower XP here (Beginner x1.0) vs 59,735 on an Expert map calculated. The safest Beginner farm and the best freeplay map. Snipers: place them so the logs don't sit between them and the track.
