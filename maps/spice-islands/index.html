Spice Islands

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Intermediate 1 lane Easiness 3/5 water: lots · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator

Mostly water, little land.

How to beat it

Key rounds
  1. R63have the Flagship plus Stronger Stimulant by R62; on Impoppable add a 2nd Destroyer if the Flagship is late.
  2. R76the 2-2-0 Village already has Grow Blocker, so regrow ceramics stay down.
  3. R90DDTs need camo + Lead + Black: Radar + MIB (2-3-0), or Brickell's Naval Tactics (water towers do Normal damage).
  4. R95Glaive Lord + MIB clean the purples; pop Naval Tactics.
  5. R98Flagship + Glaive; fire Spike Storm and Naval Tactics together.
  6. R100Mega Mines (Brickell) or Spike Storm as the BAD comes in; the Flagship planes finish it.

Easy and Medium

  • Darts on an island + Brickell, Buccaneers everywhere, Village 0-2-0 on an island, Destroyer for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • 5-6 Destroyers with camo, Stronger Stimulant and Brickell mid game, Permanent Brew late
  • Carrier Flagship. bloon.games CHIMPS: Sauda centre, Buccaneer 4-2-0 -> Flagship 5-2-0, Village 2-2-0 -> 4-2-0 -> Primary Expertise, Glaive Lord 5-0-2 on the Flagship deck before R80, Ray of Doom 5-0-2 before R90.
  • Achievement "So Spiiicey": beat ABR here with land towers only.

Follow the CHIMPS build, then the general Hard and Impoppable plan (farms, lives, prices).

CHIMPS

  • Core build: Water Flagship.
  • Spend by R40: $13,750 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250).
  • Total build $116,725 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$62,184 spare calculated.
