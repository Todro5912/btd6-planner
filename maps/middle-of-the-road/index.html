Easy and Medium

Heli Pilot early (ignores buildings), Dartling 0-0-2 on the water tower, Village for camo, Bomb/Mortar for Lead, MOAB Shove or Mauler for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Wiki: Bloon Exclusion Zone 2-0-5 on the water tower gets map-wide coverage and, with MIB, "easily handles the entire late game". BADS (tier 4) is a solid mid-game save-up. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: BADS/BEZ + Brew handle it; aim the Dartling at the start of the dense ceramic waves. R76: Grow Blocker is part of the 2-2-0/2-3-0 Village. R90/R95: MIB lets the buckshot hit DDTs; the BEZ shotgun handles the 500 camo purples. R98: BEZ + Stronger Stimulant; Eliminator on Strong. R100: MOAB Eliminator ability on the BAD after its outer layer cracks.

CHIMPS

Template B: BEZ (heavy LoS: put the Dartling on the elevated water tower, which sees over the buildings per the wiki). Spend by R40: $15,980 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250). Total build $137,320 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$41,589 spare (calc).

Placement and XP

Placement. The water tower (top-right): the one spot that ignores LoS. Put the Dartling there. Helis and Mortars anywhere. A small pool bottom-right takes water towers (Index: water yes; bloonswiki).

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 45,950 tower XP here (Beginner x1.0) vs 59,735 on an Expert map (calc from the XP formula). Bad for Snipers and most short-range towers. Good for Dartling and Heli XP.
