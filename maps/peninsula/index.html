Peninsula

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Advanced 1 lane Easiness 2/5 water: lots · line of sight: heavy Plan this map Open in Simulator Check my defence

Over 80% of placeable space is water-only; the mountain blocks LoS. Moderately hard. The central mountain "blocks line-of-sight severely".

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Subs (Airburst) and a Destroyer; Ninja on the mountaintop for camo; Destroyer for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Carrier Flagship 5-2-0 + Brickell; Perma-Spike on the top-right removable island with a 3-0-1 Brew.
  • Orca (Beast Handler water path, max power 64) is the alternative: main handler on the removable left of the mountain with Drums, camo, Relentless Glue, fed by 6 Great Whites.

CHIMPS

  • Carrier Flagship + Brickell, Perma-Spike on the top-right $500 island; alt Orca.
  • CHIMPS achievement 'Therpopylae' (pop 200,000 bloons on Peninsula).
