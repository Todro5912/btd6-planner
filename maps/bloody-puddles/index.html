Bloody Puddles

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Expert 6 lanes Easiness 1/5 water: little · line of sight: some Open in Simulator

Five paths, six entrances; the spawn point moves every round. Hardest map in BTD6 by consensus. The jeep blocks LoS; removing it drops a chinook that blocks more. The jeep costs $500 or $1,000 to remove unverified.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Lots of base Darts/Snipers spread across lanes, Subs in the puddles with Airburst, then Neva-Miss/Destroyer for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Airburst Darts subs are 'essentially required' without micro; turn into Armor Piercing Darts buffed by a 3-0-1 Alchemist for mid-game MOAB damage.
  • Giant Condor moves bloons from two spawns into one lane and drags late blimps back.

CHIMPS

  • Known opener: two Desperados at R6 (left one top-left pushed against the left lake border
  • Right one as far right as possible
  • Change targeting), then a Sub snipe, 0-0-0 Darts and Snipers
  • Mid-game Airburst -> APD with a 3-0-1 Alch
  • Giant Condor.
  • Late game: Sub Commander unverified.

How the numbers were made

  • Strategies and build orders are worked out from the numbers on this site, not play-tested. Build-order prices are Hard (x1.08), which is also what CHIMPS charges.
  • Lanes: the BTD6 Index says 6; other sources say five paths and six entrances (the Index lists only 5 lane lengths). This page uses the BTD6 Index value.
  • The Bloons Wiki infobox gives $1,000 for the jeep and its text gives $500.
