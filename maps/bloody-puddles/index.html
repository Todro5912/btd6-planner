Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Lots of base Darts/Snipers spread across lanes, Subs in the puddles with Airburst, then Neva-Miss/Destroyer for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Airburst Darts subs are 'essentially required' without micro; turn into Armor Piercing Darts buffed by a 3-0-1 Alchemist for mid-game MOAB damage. Giant Condor moves bloons from two spawns into one lane and drags late blimps back.

CHIMPS

Wiki-documented opener: two Desperados at R6 (left one top-left pushed against the left lake border; right one as far right as possible; change targeting), then a Sub snipe, 0-0-0 Darts and Snipers; mid-game Airburst -> APD with a 3-0-1 Alch; Giant Condor. Late game: Sub Commander (my addition, untested).
