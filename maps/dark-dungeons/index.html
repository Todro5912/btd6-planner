Strategies confidence: high

Easy and Medium

One Dart per bottom room (Triple Shot 0-3-0 is cost-effective here), Juggernaut 4-0-2 in left and right rooms, Overdrive at the middle MOAB lane's 2nd turnaround for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Overdrive -> Tack Zone with Embrittlement, Berserker Brew and Primary Training. Arctic Knight aims into enclosed rooms. Spiked Mines for random leaks (only two needed late). Juggernauts handle Super Ceramics on the bloon lanes in R83 and R91.

CHIMPS

Overdrive/Tack Zone right below the 2nd turnaround of the middle MOAB lane (or above the 1st turnaround of the top MOAB lane) + Embrittlement + Brew + Primary Training, 4-0-2 Juggernauts in the side rooms, Spiked Mines; Geraldo.

Placement and XP

Placement. Tack below the middle MOAB lane's 2nd turnaround; second Tack above the top MOAB lane's 1st turnaround. Juggernauts in the left and right rooms. Multiple Arctic Knights in the middle room.

XP farm. Hard Expert farm (walls). Ace or Juggernaut monos are the realistic picks.
