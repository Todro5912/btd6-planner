Dark Dungeons

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Expert 4 lanes Easiness 2/5 water: little · line of sight: heavy Plan this map Open in Simulator Check my defence

Four lanes (two bloon, one MOAB, one both) through walled rooms. Fifth-hardest map. A little water: room for 6 Subs, 4 Buccaneers or 9 Mermonkeys; where it is unverified.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • One Dart per bottom room (Triple Shot 0-3-0 is cost-effective here), Juggernaut 4-0-2 in left and right rooms, Overdrive at the middle MOAB lane's 2nd turnaround for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Overdrive -> Tack Zone with Embrittlement, Berserker Brew and Primary Training.
  • Arctic Knight aims into enclosed rooms.
  • Spiked Mines for random leaks (only two needed late).
  • Juggernauts handle Super Ceramics on the bloon lanes in R83 and R91.

CHIMPS

  • Overdrive/Tack Zone right below the 2nd turnaround of the middle MOAB lane (or above the 1st turnaround of the top MOAB lane) + Embrittlement + Brew + Primary Training, 4-0-2 Juggernauts in the side rooms, Spiked Mines
  • Geraldo.
