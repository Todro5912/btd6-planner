Strategies confidence: low

Easy and Medium

Only relevant for the Quest/custom challenges. Hit bloons on the beach: Spike Factories, Mermonkeys, Druids near the yacht.

Hard and Impoppable

The Final Harmonic (stall + buffs Magic towers, making Druid/Shinobi spam viable), Carpet of Spikes (spikes along the remaining track), Tsar Bomba as an emergency button. Tack/Sauda/Pat strategies are unreliable because the turns are right by the exits.

CHIMPS

Not applicable: this map has no medals, so there is no CHIMPS mode to beat.
