Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Towers immediately right of the (future) MOAB lane so they stay useful. 3 Darts, Boomerang 2-0-2, Dart 0-0-2 camo, Destroyer in the water right of the MOAB exit for R40. Medium: Glaive Ricochet 3-0-2 and a Village.

Hard and Impoppable

After R39, move investment to just below the first bend of the MOAB lane. Glaive Lord 5-0-2 there covers both lanes. Key rounds: R40 (Destroyer + M.O.A.R Glaives), R63 (brewed Glaives), R95-99 (Glaive Lord + Perma/Relentless Glue).

CHIMPS

Win condition: Glaive Lord placed right below the first bend of the MOAB path (thrown glaives cover both lanes, orbiting glaives shred the MOAB lane).

Placement and XP

Placement. Early: just right of the MOAB lane. Water towers immediately right of the MOAB exit. Late: Glaive Lord and supports right below the MOAB lane's first bend.

XP farm. Advanced Deflation. Short MOAB lane hurts mono farms; Boomerang (2x M.O.A.R Glaives 4-0-2 + radar, $9,395 Easy) and Destroyers are good fits.
