Muddy Puddles

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Expert 4 lanes Easiness 2/5 water: little · line of sight: clear Plan this map Open in Simulator

Four alternating lanes; the spawn sweeps left to right and back; three puddles. Very hard early game.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Subs in the puddles (Advanced Intel = global range), base Darts spread across lanes, Destroyer/Heli for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Subs (Twin Guns, Airburst, Triple Guns) early
  • Comanche Commander with Geraldo/Glue Storm/MIB late
  • Beast Handler merges for intel/repositioning (Adasaurus, Velociraptor).

CHIMPS

  • Subs with Advanced Intel early -> Comanche Commander (+Glue Storm for DDTs; MIB alternative); hero Corvus or Rosalia; First Strike for the BAD.
