Chutes

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Intermediate 2 lanes Easiness 1/5 water: little · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator Check my defence

Two very short alternating lanes; chutes block LoS; two pools. Among the hardest Intermediate maps, harder than some Advanced ones.

How to beat it

Key rounds
  1. R63Comanche Defense + Glue Strike ability on wave 2.
  2. R76Grow Blocker is in the 2-2-0 Village.
  3. R90Glue Storm de-Leads DDTs, so no MIB needed; Radar for camo.
  4. R95Comanche Commander + Glue Storm + Perma-Spike.
  5. R98Glue Storm ability + Comanche; Geraldo's Genie Bottle if bought.
  6. R100Comanche + Perma-Spike; MOAB Shove stalls blimps but not the BAD.

Easy and Medium

  • Remove both chutes when you can ($700).
  • One Dart per lane + hero, Heli Pilot, Bomb for Lead, Village, MOAB Shove for R40.
  • Spike Factories at both exits.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Comanche Commander + Glue Storm (Glue Storm lets it hit DDTs, no MIB needed
  • Beats Apache Prime vs DDTs) + Geraldo
  • Or Carrier Flagship + Brickell (Naval Tactics, Mega Mines).

Follow the CHIMPS build, then the general Hard and Impoppable plan (farms, lives, prices).

CHIMPS

  • Core build: Comanche.
  • Spend by R40: $13,455 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250).
  • Total build $134,820 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$44,089 spare calculated.
