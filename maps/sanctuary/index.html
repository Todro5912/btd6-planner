Sanctuary

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Expert 4 lanes Easiness 4/5 water: little · line of sight: clear unverified Plan this map Open in Simulator

Two entrances, four exits; six moving platforms carry towers on a 24-round cycle. A "casual" Expert, about as hard as Workshop. It has water; where is unverified.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • 3 Snipers (moving platforms don't matter to global range), a Dart, then Dartling or Destroyer for R40 (the map has water, but where is unverified
  • Check a water spot reaches the track).

Hard and Impoppable

  • BEZ on the bottom platform (shoots against the grain), saved via BADS
  • Perma-Spike on the bottom-middle platform with Set Target on where leaks end up.

CHIMPS

  • Bloon Exclusion Zone can solo late CHIMPS with Alchemist buffs, Relentless Glue, MIB/Glue Storm, Bloon Sabotage and Overclock
  • Perma-Spike on the bottom-middle platform (Set Target).
  • Corvus for experts.
