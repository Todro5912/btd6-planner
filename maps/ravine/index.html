Two alternating entrances split into four exits; bridges and walls block LoS; low water blocks water towers. Second-hardest map (Corvus has made it easier). Wiki: 3 bridges with 6 walls plus trees "significantly" block LoS; low water blocks water towers that cannot shoot over walls.

Easy and Medium

Triple Shot Darts in the bottom-left space (all 4 lanes pass around it), Sauda there, Spike Factory on the very short leftmost path.

Hard and Impoppable

Sky Shredder (missiles pass LoS blockers) with Stronger Stimulant; Prince of Darkness at the hidden spot in the centre-left trees; Spiked Mines on the leftmost path; Relentless Glue.

CHIMPS
