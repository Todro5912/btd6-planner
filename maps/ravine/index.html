Ravine

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Expert 4 lanes Easiness 1/5 water: little · line of sight: heavy Plan this map Open in Simulator

Two alternating entrances split into four exits; bridges and walls block LoS; low water blocks water towers. Second-hardest map (Corvus has made it easier). 3 bridges with 6 walls plus trees "significantly" block LoS; low water blocks water towers that cannot shoot over walls.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Triple Shot Darts in the bottom-left space (all 4 lanes pass around it), Sauda there, Spike Factory on the very short leftmost path.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Sky Shredder (missiles pass LoS blockers) with Stronger Stimulant
  • Prince of Darkness at the hidden spot in the centre-left trees
  • Spiked Mines on the leftmost path
  • Relentless Glue.

CHIMPS

  • Corvus (best map) + Spiked Mines; Sky Shredder + Stronger Stimulant + Prince of Darkness cleanup; Relentless Glue is 'especially vital'.
