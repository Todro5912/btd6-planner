Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Towers on eroding land refund 70% when sold by collapse, so just rebuild near the active lane. Subs with Advanced Intel in the water, then Destroyer for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Same as Easy but keep heroes off eroding land. Build a BADS 2-0-4 -> BEZ 2-0-5 Dartling on the permanent top-right platform. Arctic Wind platforms can save land towers at R90+.

CHIMPS

Wiki win conditions: Bloon Exclusion Zone (long mid-game tracks allow the save-up) and Carpet of Spikes 0-5-2 on the short R90+ lane, with MIB or Embrittlement for DDTs. Place as few towers as possible outside the permanent platform.

Placement and XP

Placement. Everything important on the permanent top-right platform. Subs in water with Advanced Intel so they shoot anything in range of your other towers. Arctic Wind ice to salvage land towers late.

XP farm. Advanced Deflation only (not CHIMPS) is fine here because sold-by-collapse towers refund outside CHIMPS; still, R35 and R53 collapses happen inside the Deflation window, so put the farm tower on the permanent platform.
