Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Sauda top-left of the tight loop, Dart 0-0-2 for camo, Spike Factory at the exit, Bomb 0-3-0 for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Perma-Spike as far right as possible on the top section with Jungle Drums and Brew, on Smart. Giant Condor: on 4n (straight) rounds aim the reticle at the start of the large outer loop so dragged blimps walk much farther. Watch R40/R60/R80/R100 (4n = straight, short route).

CHIMPS

Sauda R6 start (top-left of tight loop) -> Perma-Spike + Giant Condor, with Relentless Glue stuns for Sauda's bonus damage.

Placement and XP

Placement. Sauda immediately top-left of the tight loop. Perma-Spike far right on the top section. Condor reticle at the start of the big loop.

XP farm. Advanced Deflation. Round 40 and 60 are straight-line rounds (4n), so short-range farms struggle; Spike Factory and Sniper are safest.
