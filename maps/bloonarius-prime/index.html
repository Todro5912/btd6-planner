Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Darts near the entrance (both lanes share it), Heli Pilot, Village, Bomb, MOAB Shove for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Corvus, Geraldo + Comanche Commander or Snipers with Permanent Brew, Heli Pilot (flying, unlimited range). Build the R79/R89/R97 defence near the shared start, since those use the short lane. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: Comanche Defense + Glue Strike ability on wave 2. R76: Grow Blocker is in the 2-2-0 Village. R90: Glue Storm de-Leads DDTs, so no MIB needed; Radar for camo. R95: Comanche Commander + Glue Storm + Perma-Spike. R98: Glue Storm ability + Comanche; Geraldo's Genie Bottle if bought. R100: Comanche + Perma-Spike; MOAB Shove stalls blimps but not the BAD.

CHIMPS

Core build: Comanche. Spend by R40: $12,755 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250). Total build $134,120 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$44,789 spare calculated.

Placement and XP

Placement. Near the shared entrance (covers both lanes). Avoid the centre clump. Water towers in the river where it borders the composite lane.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 50,545 tower XP here (Intermediate x1.1) vs 59,735 on an Expert map calculated. Hard for its tier. Not a farm map.
