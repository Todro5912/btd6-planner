Easy and Medium

Darts + hero covering both bloon lanes, Dartling or Heli, Village, Bomb, Mauler aimed at the blimp lane for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

BEZ 2-0-5 on the far right (spread covers much of the map), with Stronger Stimulant and 4 extra Acidic Mixture Dip alchemists for DDTs it "can solo the late game"; Carpet of Spikes 2-5-0 (spikes hit blimps on both rings; trivializes DDT rounds and R100). Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: BADS/BEZ + Brew handle it; aim the Dartling at the start of the dense ceramic waves. R76: Grow Blocker is part of the 2-2-0/2-3-0 Village. R90/R95: MIB lets the buckshot hit DDTs; the BEZ shotgun handles the 500 camo purples. R98: BEZ + Stronger Stimulant; Eliminator on Strong. R100: MOAB Eliminator ability on the BAD after its outer layer cracks.

CHIMPS

Core build: BEZ. Spend by R40: $11,265 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250). Total build $138,380 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$40,529 spare calculated.

Placement and XP

Placement. BEZ on the far right. Three entrances and three exits (bloon lanes from the top-left and bottom-left, plus a MOAB lane). Remove the five cars ($250 each) that block your carries' LoS. Melt one of the two ponds ($500 each) only if you go water.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 50,545 tower XP here (Intermediate x1.1) vs 59,735 on an Expert map calculated. Hard early game: not a farm map.
