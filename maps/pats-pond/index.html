Pat's Pond

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Advanced 2 lanes Easiness 3/5 water: lots · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator Check my defence

Two lanes that split far apart in the middle; large pond.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Tack Sprayer 2-0-3 where the lanes meet, Subs in the pond, Bomb or Alchemist acid for lead, Destroyer for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Classic Sub + Village + Alchemist core: Airburst -> Triple Guns -> Armor Piercing Darts, Acid Dip/Brew alchemist for lead and speed, Sub Commander 2-0-5 late.

CHIMPS

  • A 2-0-3 Tack Sprayer solos the first few CHIMPS rounds.
  • The rest is the Sub + Village + Alch archetype with Sub Commander unverified.
