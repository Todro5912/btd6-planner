Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Darts + hero, a Dart path to Juggernaut inside the crypt (bounces), Village, Bomb, Destroyer/Mauler for R40. Don't fill the centre with early towers.

Hard and Impoppable

Ultra-Juggernaut inside the crypt with Brew (balls re-hit off walls); Prince of Darkness builds its graveyard through the wall; Carrier Flagship's planes reach outside the walls and its top platform sees over them. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: have the Flagship plus Stronger Stimulant by R62; on Impoppable add a 2nd Destroyer if the Flagship is late. R76: the 2-2-0 Village already has Grow Blocker, so regrow ceramics stay down. R90: DDTs need camo + Lead + Black: Radar + MIB (2-3-0), or Brickell's Naval Tactics (water towers do Normal damage). R95: Glaive Lord + MIB clean the purples; pop Naval Tactics. R98: Flagship + Glaive; fire Spike Storm and Naval Tactics together. R100: Mega Mines (Brickell) or Spike Storm as the BAD comes in; the Flagship planes finish it.

CHIMPS

Core build: Water Flagship (Carrier Flagship is the usual pick here; where the water spot is unverified, so check a Buccaneer fits before committing). Spend by R40: $14,000 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250). Total build $116,975 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$61,934 spare calculated.

Placement and XP

Placement. Central space for the win condition only. Category pads: Primary pad for a Juggernaut/Tack, Support pad for the Village/Spike Factory, Magic pad for Wizard/Alch, Military pad for a Mortar (a Sniper there only if it has a clear line; the crypt wall blocks most sightlines).

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 50,545 tower XP here (Intermediate x1.1) vs 59,735 on an Expert map calculated. Walls make it awkward; average for farming.
