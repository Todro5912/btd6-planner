Easy and Medium

Druid 1-3-0 (Druid of the Jungle) on Space 4 (+1 damage) early, Village, Bomb for Lead, then whatever carry you like on the buff tiles.

Hard and Impoppable

Druid of the Jungle on Space 4 simplifies the early game. Snowstorm and Glue Strike on the Discount tile (Space 7) near a Primary Mentoring village. Sun Avatars (1-damage hits) love the +1 damage spaces 16 and 20. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: The Tack Zone must be in by R59-62; Brew + Primary Training make it shred the 3 ceramic waves. R76: Grow Blocker (Village x-1-x) stops regrow. R90: DDT camo from Radar, Lead from the Alchemist's Acidic Mixture Dip (part of 4-0-0). R95: Tack Zone + Perma-Spike; purples are fine (tacks are sharp). R98: Tack Zone + Perma-Spike; Gwen's Firestorm if you use abilities. R100: Perma-Spike stack plus Tack Zone; the guide ends with ~$91k spare, so add a MOAB Eliminator if worried.

CHIMPS

Core build: Tack Zone + Perma-Spike. Spend by R40: $14,205 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250). Total build $86,515 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$92,394 spare calculated. Build: R6 Tack Shooter 1-0-1 at the tightest bend/loop (+$600, total $600); R9 Tack to 2-0-2 (+$400, total $1,000); R13 Gwendolin next to the Tack (+$785, total $1,785); R19 Spike Factory 0-0-2 near the exit (spikes hit camo: covers R24/R33) (+$1,670, total $3,455); R36 Spike Factory to 0-2-4 Deadly Spikes (+$6,810, total $10,265); R39 Tack to 2-0-4 Overdrive (the R40 MOAB answer with the spikes) (+$3,940, total $14,205); R42 Alchemist 3-0-0 Berserker Brew next to the Tack (+$2,755, total $16,960); R46 Monkey Village 3-2-0 (Primary Training + Radar Scanner + Grow Blocker) covering the Tack (+$6,640, total $23,600); R49 2nd Alchemist 4-0-0 Stronger Stimulant hugging the Tack (guide says 4-0-1) (+$5,835, total $29,435); R59 Tack to 2-0-5 The Tack Zone (before R63) (+$21,600, total $51,035); R78 Spike Factory to 0-2-5 Perma-Spike (+$32,400, total $83,435); R79 1st Alchemist to 4-0-0 (+$3,080, total $86,515). Map note: put the Tack on a +1 damage or attack-speed tile when one is open, because Tack Zone fires many 1-damage tacks unverified.

Placement and XP

Placement. Put carries on the buff tiles: 1-damage attackers (Tack, Sun Avatar) on +1 damage, abilities/support on the discount tile, ranged towers on +15% range. Which tiles open on which round is unverified.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 45,950 tower XP here (Beginner x1.0) vs 59,735 on an Expert map calculated. Buff tiles make it easier than it looks. Snipers hampered by fences/trees.
