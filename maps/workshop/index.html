Strategies confidence: high

Easy and Medium

Three Darts in fairly precise spots for the first rounds, then a Spike Factory at the shared exit and Sauda. Use both gizmos on hard rounds (they're nearly free). Reverse mode makes the map harder.

Hard and Impoppable

Spiked Mines carry the mid-game, Perma-Spike trivialises the late game, Super Glue (stronger than Icicle Impale since v41) stalls for the spikes.

CHIMPS

Wiki: Spike Factories (Spiked Mines -> Perma-Spike) + Sauda + Super Glue; use the Reverse Motor/Track Extensions on R40, R63, R78, R95, R98.
