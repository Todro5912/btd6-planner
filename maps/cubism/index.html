Cubism

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Beginner 1 lane Easiness 5/5 water: little · line of sight: clear Open in Simulator

Many track intersections left of centre. Among the easiest maps; easy CHIMPS.

How to beat it

Key rounds
  1. R63The Tack Zone must be in by R59–62; Brew + Primary Training make it shred the 3 ceramic waves.
  2. R76Grow Blocker (Village x-1-x) stops regrow.
  3. R90DDT camo from Radar, Lead from the Alchemist's Acidic Mixture Dip (part of 4-0-0).
  4. R95Tack Zone + Perma-Spike; purples are fine (tacks are sharp).
  5. R98Tack Zone + Perma-Spike; Gwen's Firestorm if you use abilities.
  6. R100Perma-Spike stack plus Tack Zone; the guide ends with ~$91k spare, so add a MOAB Eliminator if worried.

Easy and Medium

  • Tack 2-0-3 in one of the small central shapes, plus Gwen.
  • Village for camo, Alchemist 3-0-0, Overdrive by R40.
  • Medium: farms + Tack Zone if unlocked.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Same Tack Zone route; on Impoppable put 2 farms on the far right and go Tack Zone by ~R55.

Follow the CHIMPS build, then the general Hard and Impoppable plan (farms, lives, prices).

CHIMPS

  • Core build: Tack Zone + Perma-Spike.
  • Spend by R40: $14,205 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250).
  • Total build $86,515 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$92,394 spare calculated.
