Quad

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Expert 4 lanes Easiness 1/5 water: little · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator

Four overlapping lanes active together; trusses make bloons under them immune. True Expert, 3rd hardest. Central pond for water towers; 4 trusses block LoS and make bloons (blimps too) immune underneath.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Two clusters, top and bottom.
  • Two base Desperados can beat the first rounds; then Spike Factories, Sauda, Boomerang.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Spiked Balls/Mines each tank two lanes (making Quad a 2-lane map); Glaive Lord (strong since v43); Giant Condor halves the lanes to watch.

CHIMPS

  • Two Spiked Mines (one per half) + Sauda + Glaive Lord + Giant Condor.
  • Feb 2025 chart comment: 'Quad is Condor's best map'.
