Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Two clusters, top and bottom. Two base Desperados can beat the first rounds; then Spike Factories, Sauda, Boomerang.

Hard and Impoppable

Spiked Balls/Mines each tank two lanes (making Quad a 2-lane map); Glaive Lord (strong since v43); Giant Condor halves the lanes to watch.

CHIMPS

Wiki: two Spiked Mines (one per half) + Sauda + Glaive Lord + Giant Condor. Feb 2025 chart comment: 'Quad is Condor's best map'.
