Cracked

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Intermediate 1 lane Easiness 5/5 water: little · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator Check my defence

Central rock blocks LoS. One of the easiest Intermediate maps. Water only after removing the rock.

How to beat it

Key rounds
  1. R63have the Flagship plus Stronger Stimulant by R62; on Impoppable add a 2nd Destroyer if the Flagship is late.
  2. R76the 2-2-0 Village already has Grow Blocker, so regrow ceramics stay down.
  3. R90DDTs need camo + Lead + Black: Radar + MIB (2-3-0), or Brickell's Naval Tactics (water towers do Normal damage).
  4. R95Glaive Lord + MIB clean the purples; pop Naval Tactics.
  5. R98Flagship + Glaive; fire Spike Storm and Naval Tactics together.
  6. R100Mega Mines (Brickell) or Spike Storm as the BAD comes in; the Flagship planes finish it.

Easy and Medium

  • Darts + Quincy right of the rock, Village, Bomb, Mauler/Destroyer.
  • Remove the rock when you have spare cash.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Heli Pilots and Comanche Defense/Commander use the full track; The Bloon Solver right of the rock covers multiple sections and pairs with Comanche.

Follow the CHIMPS build, then the general Hard and Impoppable plan (farms, lives, prices).

CHIMPS

  • Known route (Quincy, rock removed, Destroyer -> Flagship 5-2-0, Village 2-2-0 -> Primary Expertise, Glaive 5-0-2 and Cripple MOAB 5-0-2 on the ship).
  • Round timings calculated; Cripple MOAB ($46.9k) didn't fit before R100 alongside the rest, so a Spike Factory replaces it.
  • Custom route.
  • Spend by R40: $13,825 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250).
  • Total build $136,510 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$42,399 spare calculated.
