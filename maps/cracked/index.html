Easy and Medium

Darts + Quincy right of the rock, Village, Bomb, Mauler/Destroyer. Remove the rock when you have spare cash.

Hard and Impoppable

Wiki: Heli Pilots and Comanche Defense/Commander use the full track; The Bloon Solver right of the rock covers multiple sections and pairs with Comanche. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: have the Flagship plus Stronger Stimulant by R62; on Impoppable add a 2nd Destroyer if the Flagship is late. R76: the 2-2-0 Village already has Grow Blocker, so regrow ceramics stay down. R90: DDTs need camo + Lead + Black: Radar + MIB (2-3-0), or Brickell's Naval Tactics (water towers do Normal damage). R95: Glaive Lord + MIB clean the purples; pop Naval Tactics. R98: Flagship + Glaive; fire Spike Storm and Naval Tactics together. R100: Mega Mines (Brickell) or Spike Storm as the BAD comes in; the Flagship planes finish it.

CHIMPS

Proven/named route: bloon.games "Cracked CHIMPS Quicky" route (Quincy, rock removed, Destroyer -> Flagship 5-2-0, Village 2-2-0 -> Primary Expertise, Glaive 5-0-2 and Cripple MOAB 5-0-2 on the ship). Round timings are calc; Cripple MOAB ($46.9k) didn't fit before R100 alongside the rest, so a Spike Factory replaces it. Custom route. Spend by R40: $13,825 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250). Total build $136,510 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$42,399 spare (calc).

Placement and XP

Placement. Long-range towers right of the rock (cover several sections). Buccaneer in the revealed water; small towers on the Flagship deck.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 50,545 tower XP here (Intermediate x1.1) vs 59,735 on an Expert map (calc from the XP formula). Easy and long: a good Intermediate farm. Snipers good right of the rock.
