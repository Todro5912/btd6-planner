Spillway

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Advanced 4 lanes Easiness 3/5 water: little · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator Check my defence

Four lanes active together meeting at a central loop; one central barrel water spot. Not especially hard.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Ninja on the central barrel, Wizards for the loop (Wall of Fire hits them several times), Mortar aimed at the loop.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Pop and Awe 2-5-0 with Striker and Relentless Glue (may need one valve removed).
  • Archmage 5-2-0 is the other carry.
  • Carrier Flagship sees over valves.

CHIMPS

  • Start with a Ninja on the barrel (then 2-0-1), then Pop and Awe 2-5-0 + Striker + Relentless Glue.
  • Alternatives: Archmage 5-2-0, Carrier Flagship, TBO aimed mid-map.
