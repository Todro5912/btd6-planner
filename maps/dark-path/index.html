Dark Path

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Advanced 1 lane Easiness 4/5 water: none · line of sight: some Open in Simulator

Inverted Park Path; 8 cave walls (4 per cave) knock down for $250/$500/$1,000/$1,000 per cave, $5,500 for all calculated. Easy tier, about equal to Another Brick and Off The Coast.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Mortar aimed at the middle loops from R1–6, 2 Darts on the high platform, Bomb for black/lead backup, Mortar 2-3-0 by R40 plus a Bomb 0-3-0.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Artillery Battery 2-4-0 sweeps the mid-game and saves into Pop and Awe.
  • Relentless Glue stuns add damage.
  • Keep walls intact if you go Ultra-Juggernaut.
  • Key rounds: R40 (AB or 2-3-0 + Dart), R63 (AB + Striker ability), R100 (Spike Storm ability).

CHIMPS

  • Win conditions: Artillery Battery -> Pop and Awe 2-5-0 with Striker Jones and Relentless Glue, plus Perma-Spike for strays and a Spike Storm for the R100 BAD.
  • Alternative: Ultra-Juggernaut on low ground with walls intact.

Where to place

  • Mortar targets the middle loops.
  • Limited-range towers and supports on the high platform above the centre.
  • Perma-Spike at the bottom-left exit.
  • 8 cave walls, 4 per cave ($250/$500/$1,000/$1,000 each cave; $5,500 for all calculated).
  • Keep them if you use Ultra-Juggernaut bounces.

  • Removables: the BTD6 Index says 0 objects, $0; other sources say 8 cave walls, $5,500 in total. This page uses Bloons Wiki.
