X Factor

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Advanced 4 lanes Easiness 1/5 water: none · line of sight: clear Plan this map Open in Simulator Check my defence

Four entrances and four exits; each entrance feeds both paths, so 8 routes (14.5-21.0 RBS Hard); the spawn corners rotate. Hardest Advanced map by consensus.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Base Snipers everywhere, then Snipers 2-0-2 and 0-1-2, Bomb Mauler for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Snipers early, Maim MOAB to stall blimps for Psi, Carpet of Spikes late.

CHIMPS

  • Snipers early -> Maim MOAB stalls -> Carpet of Spikes (DDTs easy, ZOMG damage map-wide, trivialises the BAD) with Psi.

How the numbers were made

  • Lanes: the BTD6 Index says 4; other sources say our earlier 8 = routes (4 entrances x both paths). This page uses the BTD6 Index value.
