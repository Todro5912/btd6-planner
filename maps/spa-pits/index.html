Spa Pits

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Beginner 1 lane Easiness 5/5 water: little · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator

Easier remake of Tar Pits; five hot springs hold water towers. Among the easiest maps. The five caves block LoS; hot springs take water towers.

How to beat it

Key rounds
  1. R63have the Flagship plus Stronger Stimulant by R62; on Impoppable add a 2nd Destroyer if the Flagship is late.
  2. R76the 2-2-0 Village already has Grow Blocker, so regrow ceramics stay down.
  3. R90DDTs need camo + Lead + Black: Radar + MIB (2-3-0), or Brickell's Naval Tactics (water towers do Normal damage).
  4. R95Glaive Lord + MIB clean the purples; pop Naval Tactics.
  5. R98Flagship + Glaive; fire Spike Storm and Naval Tactics together.
  6. R100Mega Mines (Brickell) or Spike Storm as the BAD comes in; the Flagship planes finish it.

Easy and Medium

  • Buccaneers and Subs in the springs, Village by the central pool, Destroyer for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Carrier Flagship 5-2-0 + Brickell; Bloontonium Reactor in the middle of the central pool for decamo; Energizer; Orca (merge Great Whites to Power 64).
  • Save one spot next to the central pool for a Jungle Drums village.
  • The "early CHIMPS is impossible" warning you may see is about the secret harder layout.

Follow the CHIMPS build, then the general Hard and Impoppable plan (farms, lives, prices).

CHIMPS

  • Known route (Buccaneer -> Flagship, Sub 4-2-0 -> Energizer, Glaive 5-0-2, Cripple 5-0-2; no round numbers given).
  • Round timings calculated.
  • A cheaper Spike Factory replaces the final Cripple MOAB, because the cash doesn't reach $46.9k by R95.
  • Custom route.
  • Spend by R40: $11,645 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250).
  • Total build $143,450 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$35,459 spare calculated.
