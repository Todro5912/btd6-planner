Easy and Medium

Buccaneers and Subs in the springs, Village by the central pool, Destroyer for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Carrier Flagship 5-2-0 + Brickell; Bloontonium Reactor in the middle of the central pool for decamo; Energizer; Orca (merge Great Whites to Power 64). Save one spot next to the central pool for a Jungle Drums village. The "early CHIMPS is impossible" warning you may see is about the secret harder layout. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: have the Flagship plus Stronger Stimulant by R62; on Impoppable add a 2nd Destroyer if the Flagship is late. R76: the 2-2-0 Village already has Grow Blocker, so regrow ceramics stay down. R90: DDTs need camo + Lead + Black: Radar + MIB (2-3-0), or Brickell's Naval Tactics (water towers do Normal damage). R95: Glaive Lord + MIB clean the purples; pop Naval Tactics. R98: Flagship + Glaive; fire Spike Storm and Naval Tactics together. R100: Mega Mines (Brickell) or Spike Storm as the BAD comes in; the Flagship planes finish it.

Known route (Buccaneer -> Flagship, Sub 4-2-0 -> Energizer, Glaive 5-0-2, Cripple 5-0-2; no round numbers given). Round timings calculated. Placement and XP

Placement. Water towers in the hot springs on bends; Sub/Reactor in the middle of the central pool; Village next to the central pool.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 45,950 tower XP here (Beginner x1.0) vs 59,735 on an Expert map calculated. Among the easiest maps (4th easiest): very good for Buccaneer/Sub Deflation. Caves block some sight lines for Snipers.
