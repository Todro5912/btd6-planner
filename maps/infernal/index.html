Infernal

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Expert 2 lanes Easiness 5/5 water: little · line of sight: clear Plan this map Open in Simulator

Two alternating lanes (odd rounds top, even bottom), 19.3 RBS each: the longest of any Expert map (next: Glacial Trail 17.7); little space; farms only fit on the side islands. Easiest Expert map, especially for CHIMPS. No LoS blockers. Six floating islands: 4 land and 2 water pools that fit 3 water towers each. Elite Sniper and Elite Defender have both soloed CHIMPS here.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • 2-3 Snipers + Darts, Sniper 0-1-x for camo, 2-x-x for lead, Full Auto for R40.
  • Any balanced team wins.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Sniper, Heli or Dartling carries (all suit alternating lanes).
  • Comanche Commander (Village at the top of the island, Heli as high as possible, room for an Alchemist) with Glue Storm and Geraldo's Sharpening Stone
  • Or BEZ with Stronger Stimulant, Overclock and MIB.

CHIMPS

  • Known strategy: Perma-Brewed Sniper army: save with a 0-5-2 Elite Sniper and a 0-2-5 Elite Defender (Jungle Drums + Stronger Stimulant) on the right island
  • After Permanent Brew, spam 0-2-4 Snipers on the right island and 0-3-2 on the left.
  • Alternatives: Comanche Commander or BEZ.
  • This is the ideal Sniper-XP CHIMPS map.
