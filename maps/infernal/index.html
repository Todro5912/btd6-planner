Two alternating lanes (odd rounds top, even bottom), 19.3 RBS each: the longest of any Expert map (next: Glacial Trail 17.7); little space; farms only fit on the side islands. Easiest Expert map, especially for CHIMPS. No LoS blockers. Six floating islands: 4 land and 2 water pools that fit 3 water towers each. Elite Sniper and Elite Defender have both soloed CHIMPS here.

Strategies confidence: high

Easy and Medium

2-3 Snipers + Darts, Sniper 0-1-x for camo, 2-x-x for lead, Full Auto for R40. Any balanced team wins.

Hard and Impoppable

Sniper, Heli or Dartling carries (all suit alternating lanes). Comanche Commander (Village at the top of the island, Heli as high as possible, room for an Alchemist) with Glue Storm and Geraldo's Sharpening Stone; or BEZ with Stronger Stimulant, Overclock and MIB.

CHIMPS

Known strategy: Perma-Brewed Sniper army: save with a 0-5-2 Elite Sniper and a 0-2-5 Elite Defender (Jungle Drums + Stronger Stimulant) on the right island; after Permanent Brew, spam 0-2-4 Snipers on the right island and 0-3-2 on the left. Alternatives: Comanche Commander or BEZ. This is the ideal Sniper-XP CHIMPS map.

Placement and XP

Placement. Right island: Perma-Brew Alchemist, Village and the 0-2-4 Snipers. Left island: 0-3-2 Snipers. Support Chinook can move the Perma-Brew if needed.

XP farm. Top-2 Expert Deflation map and the best Sniper map (longest Expert lanes: 19.3 RBS on each lane; next is Glacial Trail at 17.7; no LoS blockers; has water). Use 2x Sniper 2-0-4 + Village 0-2-0 Radar ($19,410 Easy; ~84.9% share = ~50,700 Sniper XP per run calculated) plus a cheap hero (Quincy/Etienne, no XP share). A fixed Mortar Signal Flare only covers one of the two alternating lanes, so it is not a safe camo answer here. Elite Sniper and Elite Defender have each soloed CHIMPS on Infernal, so Sniper damage alone is proven enough on this map.
