Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

2 Darts at the first bend, a Sub in each pothole, then Ninja 2-0-1 for camo by R24, a Bomb Shooter for R28 lead and a 3-2-0 Destroyer in a pothole for the R40 MOAB. On Medium add a 2-2-0 Village and push the Buccaneer to Aircraft Carrier (4-2-0) for R50-60.

Hard and Impoppable

Same core, but get a 2-0-0 farm (or Benjamin) going by R25-30 on Impoppable. Key rounds: R28 lead (Destroyer's Hot Shot crosspath covers it), R40 MOAB (Destroyer + Airburst Sub), R63 ceramic rush (Perma-Spike 2-0-4 near the exit), R78 camo ceramics (Village radar). Buy Carrier Flagship 5-2-0 before R80; on Impoppable a Prince of Darkness 0-2-5 is a beginner-friendly second wincon that cleans leaks over the walls.

CHIMPS

Wiki-named win conditions: Carrier Flagship 5-2-0 + Admiral Brickell, with Perma-Spike near the exit (with Jungle Drums and a 3-0-1 Brew). Alternative: Prince of Darkness + Ezili. No Index record could be read.

Placement and XP

Placement. Subs and Brickell in the two potholes. Destroyer/Flagship in a pothole (it sees over walls). Perma-Spike at the exit with the Village and Alchemist touching it. Pay the two $1,000 pallets only if you need room on the left.

XP farm. Advanced Deflation (55,140 base XP). Water is tiny, so a mono-Buccaneer farm is cramped. Best mono towers: Ace (Neva-Miss ignores walls) and Sniper only where sightlines exist; Spike Factory at the exit works well.
