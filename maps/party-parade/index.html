Party Parade

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Advanced 2 lanes Easiness 2/5 water: none · line of sight: heavy Open in Simulator

Two fully separated lanes; a wide central wall blocks placement and LoS, so no single range circle covers both lanes. One of the harder Advanced maps, "debatably a casual Expert".

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Mirror defences: 1-2 Darts per lane, Dart 0-0-2 camo on each side, Bomb on each side, MOAB Mauler per side for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Prince of Darkness with a range buff (Village or Etienne) on each side of the wall (one tower cannot cover both lanes: the wall blocks placement and LoS)
  • Spirit of the Forest for the mid-game
  • Carpet of Spikes or Aces for the end.

CHIMPS

  • Psi + Prince of Darkness (with a range buff) + Carpet of Spikes (covers both lanes, beats DDTs, trivialises the BAD).

Where to place

  • The wide central wall blocks both placement and LoS, so nothing can sit in the middle and no single Wizard or Village circle reaches both lanes.
  • Mirror the defence instead: a Prince of Darkness with its own range buff on each side, plus both-lane tools (Psi, Carpet of Spikes, Aces).
  • Mirror early towers on each side.
