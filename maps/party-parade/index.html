Easy and Medium

Mirror defences: 1-2 Darts per lane, Dart 0-0-2 camo on each side, Bomb on each side, MOAB Mauler per side for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Prince of Darkness with a range buff (Village or Etienne) on each side of the wall (one tower cannot cover both lanes: the wall blocks placement and LoS); Spirit of the Forest for the mid-game; Carpet of Spikes or Aces for the end.

CHIMPS

Placement and XP

Placement. The wide central wall blocks both placement and LoS (bloonswiki), so nothing can sit in the middle and no single Wizard or Village circle reaches both lanes. Mirror the defence instead: a Prince of Darkness with its own range buff on each side, plus both-lane tools (Psi, Carpet of Spikes, Aces). Mirror early towers on each side.

XP farm. Advanced Deflation; two short separated lanes make mono farms awkward. Spike Factory or Ace are the best monos.
