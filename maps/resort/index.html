Strategies confidence: high

Easy and Medium

Sauda in the top-right space + a Dart. Tack 2-0-3 next to her, Bomb for Lead, Village for camo, Overdrive or Mauler for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Wiki picks: Overdrive -> Tack Zone at the top of the top-right space with Brew and Geraldo; Embrittlement in the top-right loop; Artillery Battery -> Pop and Awe on the top-right loop with Striker + Relentless Glue, covering the BAD with First Strike or Spike Storm. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: Tack Zone (R60) + Sauda + Brew at the loop. R76: Embrittlement (+1 damage) and Grow Blocker in the Village. R90: Embrittlement strips Lead and camo off DDTs. R95: Tack Zone + Sauda; keep Embrittlement in range of the loop. R98: Relentless Glue stuns + Sauda's abilities. R100: Perma-Spike + Sauda + Tack Zone; add a Spike Storm or MOAB Eliminator if short on single-target.

Placement and XP

Placement. Top-right enclosed space, ideally a corner so others fit. Tack Zone at the top of that space (bloons stay in the kill zone). Water towers in the pool.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 45,950 tower XP here (Beginner x1.0) vs 59,735 on an Expert map (calc from the XP formula). Long and easy: one of the best Beginner Deflation maps. Close the umbrellas for Snipers.
