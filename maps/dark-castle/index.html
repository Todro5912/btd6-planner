Strategies confidence: high

Easy and Medium

Stack everything around the convergence before the exit: 3 Darts, Druid 1-0-3, Dart 0-0-2 for camo, Spike Factory near the exit, Bomb 0-3-0 for R40. Reverse: bloons enter at the single exit, so defend that spot first.

Hard and Impoppable

Perma-Spike at the convergent exit + Avatar of Wrath 0-1-5 fed by ~6 Poplusts, Stronger Stimulant and an MIB village for DDTs. Primary Expertise 5-2-0 village is the alternative (Icicle Impale, Bomb Blitz, Tack Zone under the track).

CHIMPS

Wiki: Avatar of Wrath 0-1-5 with 6 Poplusts, Stronger Stimulant and MIB, paired with Perma-Spike at the convergent exit; Obyn/Geraldo. CHIMPS section on the wiki is empty, so round timings are my calc. The plan fits 5 Poplusts (druids #2-#6) plus the Avatar; chapter 02 says the Poplust buff stacks up to 5, so a 6th mainly adds damage.
