Tricky Tracks

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Expert 6 lanes Easiness 4/5 water: none · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator Check my defence

Six very short paths on three train tracks; bloons rush to the middle, pause, then rush out. Stop-light train gimmick: $2,500, and the price goes up with each use. One of the less mechanically annoying Expert maps.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Darts at the track centres (bloons pause there), a Wizard with Wall of Fire at a centre, buy the train for R40/R50.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Archmage 5-2-0 with range buffs (Deadeye, Bounty Hunter, Village) to seek bloons on all three tracks
  • Bloon Solver with Primary Mentoring for Super Ceramics/weak blimps.

CHIMPS

  • Archmage 5-2-0 + range buffs (Desperado Deadeye/Bounty Hunter (its bounty cash is disabled in CHIMPS
  • Buy it only for the buff), Village, Etienne) + The Bloon Solver with Primary Mentoring
  • Use the train on dense rounds.
  • Older guides for this map are outdated, so confidence is low.
