Strategies confidence: low

Easy and Medium

Darts at the track centres (bloons pause there), a Wizard with Wall of Fire at a centre, buy the train for R40/R50.

Hard and Impoppable

Archmage 5-2-0 with range buffs (Deadeye, Bounty Hunter, Village) to seek bloons on all three tracks; Bloon Solver with Primary Mentoring for Super Ceramics/weak blimps.

CHIMPS

Wiki: Archmage 5-2-0 + range buffs (Desperado Deadeye/Bounty Hunter (its bounty cash is disabled in CHIMPS; buy it only for the buff), Village, Etienne) + The Bloon Solver with Primary Mentoring; use the train on dense rounds. Wiki section is flagged outdated, so confidence is low.
