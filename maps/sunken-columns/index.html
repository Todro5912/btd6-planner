Placement and XP

Placement. Both big bends (top and bottom) where the lanes intersect. Two ponds (one per central basin) drain for $1,000 each ($2,000 for both). Land towers inside a basin are LoS-blocked except on its river strips.

XP farm. Advanced Deflation; Boomerang mono on a bend is efficient.
