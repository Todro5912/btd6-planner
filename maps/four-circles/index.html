Four Circles

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Beginner 1 lane Easiness 5/5 water: little · line of sight: clear Plan this map Open in Simulator

Loops around four circles; two pools. One of the least difficult maps.

How to beat it

Key rounds
  1. R63M.O.A.R Glaives + Primary Training; MOAB Press bounces the R64 MOABs.
  2. R76Grow Blocker is in the 3-2-0 Village.
  3. R90Red Hot Rangs (x-x-2) pop Lead, Radar gives camo, rangs are sharp so Black is fine.
  4. R95Glaive Lord + MOAB Domination.
  5. R98MOAB Domination's press attack hits the BFBs/ZOMGs across most of the map.
  6. R100MOAB Domination with Primary Training, Radar and Stronger Stimulant handles the BAD; Spike Storm as backup.

Easy and Medium

  • Anything works.
  • Boomerang 3-0-2 at the centre, Village for camo, Bomb for Lead, MOAB Press or Mauler for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

  • MOAB Domination at the centre "trivializes the entire late-game"; Glaive Lord at the centre cleans clumps.

Follow the CHIMPS build, then the general Hard and Impoppable plan (farms, lives, prices).

CHIMPS

  • Core build: MOAB Domination + Glaive.
  • Spend by R40: $14,975 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250).
  • Total build $125,515 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$53,394 spare calculated.
