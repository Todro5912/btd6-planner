Castle Revenge

v57.4 · updated 2026-11-05
Advanced 4 lanes Easiness 1/5 water: little · line of sight: some Plan this map Open in Simulator

Four entrances (wheel and water spawns, top and bottom) and two exits; six path variants 14.5-16.8 RBS; bloons cannot be damaged until they pass the wheels; the $500 pressure valve is a reusable gizmo. Only Erosion and X Factor are rated harder among Advanced maps.

How to beat it

Easy and Medium

  • Separate defences per lane.
  • One Dart line on each last bend (Juggernaut 4-0-2), a Dart 0-0-2 for camo, Bomb 0-3-0 MOAB Mauler for R40.
  • Use the $500 valve on dense rounds.

Hard and Impoppable

  • Juggernaut 4-0-2 on the last bend of each side, Spike Factory building toward Carpet of Spikes, Giant Condor with its drop-off set on the **bottom** lane entrance.
  • Key rounds: R40 (2 Juggs + Psi), R63 (valve + Spike Storm), R95–100 (Carpet trivialises the BAD).

CHIMPS

  • Win condition: Carpet of Spikes (covers both lanes, beats DDTs, cracks ZOMGs, makes the R100 BAD free so you can spend on R96–99) + Giant Condor to merge blimps into one lane + 4-0-2 Juggernauts on each last bend.

Where to place

  • Juggernauts near the last bend on each side.
  • Condor drop-off on the bottom lane entrance (the default top-lane drop-off misbehaves).
  • Central platform for anything that must see both lanes.
  • Layout: 4 entrances (wheel and water spawns, top and bottom), 2 exits, six path variants of 14.5-16.8 RBS
  • The $500 valve is a reusable gizmo, not a removable.

Removable: Pressure valve ($500 Medium): reusable gizmo (knockback, 75 s cooldown), not a one-time clearance
