Strategies confidence: high

Easy and Medium

Druid of the Jungle 1-3-0 (vines on long alternating lanes), Dart 0-0-2 for camo, then Neva-Miss Ace 2-0-3 for R40+. Medium: Village 2-2-0 + Brew.

Hard and Impoppable

Known route: Druid of the Jungle -> Neva-Miss -> Spectre (with Berserker Brew and a 2-2-0 Village) -> Flying Fortress. Spectre holds to the mid-80s with occasional Rosalia abilities. Key rounds: R40 (Neva-Miss + DotJ), R63 (Spectre or brewed Neva), R85-95 (Fortress).

CHIMPS

Known route: Druid of the Jungle -> Neva-Miss -> Spectre -> Flying Fortress 2-0-5 with Rosalia (Fortress on R87-88 thanks to her discount; without the discount the cash only gets there on R95 calculated). Alternatives: Pop and Awe or The Biggest One targeting the active lane's first bend, then the central chokepoint.

Placement and XP

Placement. Bottom-centre space and directly below the centre see both lanes. Short-range towers (Sauda) in the bends below the central tree. Perma-Spike at the top-middle spot right below the chokepoint, on Smart. Lots of LoS blockers: check a Sniper or Dartling sight line in Sandbox before committing.

XP farm. Advanced Deflation. Aces (Neva-Miss) are the natural mono farm: 2x Neva-Miss 2-0-3 + radar Village costs $12,135 Easy calculated.
