Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

3 Darts in the section top-right of centre (both lanes close), Spike Factory for leaks, Bomb for lead, Bomb 0-3-0 for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Perma-Spike tanks both lanes. Giant Condor with its reticle on the top lane makes most rounds single-lane. Symphonic Resonance can pull one lane's bloons toward the other. Watch targeting: towers favour the bottom lane and let top-lane bloons get far.

CHIMPS

Perma-Spike + Giant Condor (reticle on the top lane) + Corvus, with Symphonic Resonance as an extra stall.
