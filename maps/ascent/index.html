Primary Expertise

Strategies confidence: low

Easy and Medium

Dart + Sub start, Ninja 2-0-1 for camo, Bomb Shooter for lead, Destroyer 3-2-0 (water) for R40. Place damage where both paths are closest. Medium: add Village 2-2-0 and Aircraft Carrier.

Hard and Impoppable

Farm 1-2 farms from R20. Key rounds R40 (Destroyer), R63 (Village + brewed Destroyer/Carrier), R78 (camo; radar). Carrier Flagship covers both paths because its planes are global.

CHIMPS

A general plan built on a Carrier Flagship (global planes cover both simultaneous paths) unverified. Treat as untested.
