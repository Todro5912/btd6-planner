Strategies confidence: med

Easy and Medium

Heli Pilot or Wizards (Guided Magic sees over hedges). Dart 0-0-2 for camo, Bomb 0-2-0 for Lead, MOAB Shove Heli or Mauler for R40.

Hard and Impoppable

Top picks: Ultra-Juggernaut 5-2-0 on the 3rd space (sees through a hedge gap, balls bounce off walls) with Brew and camo; Comanche Commander + Geraldo + Glue Storm; Prince of Darkness; Archmage behind the right hedge. Build: follow the CHIMPS order below. On Hard (R3-80, 100 lives) you can stop at the R80 ZOMG, and farms are allowed: 2 Banana Farms 2-3-0 (Monkey Bank) between R13 and R30 make it comfortable. On Impoppable (R6-100, 1 life, prices x1.2 = about 11% above CHIMPS/Hard) use the same order plus 2-3 farms by R30 (sell them around R80 for 70% back) and your Monkey Knowledge. Key rounds: R63: Comanche Defense + Glue Strike ability on wave 2. R76: Grow Blocker is in the 2-2-0 Village. R90: Glue Storm de-Leads DDTs, so no MIB needed; Radar for camo. R95: Comanche Commander + Glue Storm + Perma-Spike. R98: Glue Storm ability + Comanche; Geraldo's Genie Bottle if bought. R100: Comanche + Perma-Spike; MOAB Shove stalls blimps but not the BAD.

CHIMPS

Core build: Comanche. Spend by R40: $12,755 (cash available at the start of R40: $16,250). Total build $134,120 of $178,909 earned by the end of R100, leaving ~$44,789 spare calculated.

Placement and XP

Placement. 3rd space from either side where a tower sees through a crack in the hedge (Juggernaut). Archmage behind the right hedge near the end. Helis ignore walls entirely.

XP farm. Deflation (R31-60, $20,000, Easy prices) pays about 45,950 tower XP here (Beginner x1.0) vs 59,735 on an Expert map calculated. Bad for Snipers (hedges). Fine for Heli and Wizard XP.
