Check my defence

Mid-game? Tap the towers you have. It tells you what will leak in the next 15 rounds and the cheapest fix.

About this data

Each tower's crosspath is checked against the pop model: what it can damage at all, plus support such as Village Radar, MIB and Alchemist acid (assumed in range). Fortified bloons only have more HP, so they are never flagged.

When every bloon type is covered, the leak check estimates whether your towers pop enough, from each tower's damage, pierce and attack speed on your map's track. Pick your map: without one it uses a typical track, and water towers are allowed. It is an estimate: placement and abilities change it.

Fixes list your own towers' upgrades first, then new towers, then support, priced for the mode you picked. A hero fix means a level: you can buy hero levels with cash in the game.
