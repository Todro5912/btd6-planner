Check my defence

Mid-game? Tap the towers you have. It tells you what will leak in the next 15 rounds and the cheapest fix.

About this data

Each tower's crosspath is checked against the pop model: what it can damage at all, plus support such as Village Radar, MIB and Alchemist acid (assumed in range). Fortified bloons only have more HP, so they are never flagged. A tower that can hit a bloon may still not do enough damage.

Fixes list your own towers' upgrades first, then new towers, then support, priced for the mode you picked.
