About this advice

End crosspaths come from each tower's best crosspaths on its guide page and the map page's own plan. They are picked together so your team can hit every bloon property the mode sends and the total fits the cash the mode pays by about 88% of the way through. When cash is short, a cheaper tier of the same path is allowed.

The build order is played round by round with the Simulator's own rules: its cash (leaked bloons pay no pop cash), its lives and its leak check. Each bloon type gets an answer by the round it first shows up. When a round coming up would leak, the order buys damage first: the cheapest buy from your picks that stops it, more tiers or another copy, saving up when it can pay in time.

When nothing your picks can afford holds a round, it says so and shows where it breaks and why. The leak check is an estimate: placement, abilities and targeting change it, and from R76 it over-predicts leaks. Calculated from the site's data, not play-tested.
