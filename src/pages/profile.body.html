My profile

Tell the site what you have in your game: unlocked upgrades and Paragons, the heroes you own, your Monkey Knowledge and your Insta Monkeys. The Advisor, Simulator, My goals and the XP calculator all use it. Saved in this browser only.

About this data

The Monkey Knowledge trees hold all 134 nodes from the game files (version 56.0): names, descriptions, point and Monkey Money costs, and prerequisites. The rows and arrows follow the game's point gates; the exact screen positions are ours, because the game files don't hold them.

Nodes with a yellow dot change what the Simulator and Advisor work out: More Cash, Bonus Monkey and Bonus Glue Gunner, Hero Favors, Empowered Heroes, the hero and tower XP nodes, Better Sell Deals, Flat Pack Buildings, the tower and upgrade discounts, Trade Agreements and More Valuable Bananas. Percent discounts come off before the difficulty price, flat ones after it. That order is not yet tested in game.

Big Bloon Sabotage, Veteran Monkey Training and the tower attack nodes are saved for the leak check, which doesn't use them yet. Modes that ignore Monkey Knowledge, such as CHIMPS, switch all of it off.

Insta Monkeys are placed for free in the Simulator and the Advisor, and are blocked in modes that ban them. Unlock costs, hero unlock levels and Monkey Money prices come from the tower and hero data.

Everything is stored in this browser (localStorage). Use the backup code to move it to another device.
