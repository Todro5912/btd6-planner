Setup

Best real method: Impoppable (or Hard) on an Expert map with a heavy farming plan - farms typically hold a large share of invested cash from R20-R60.

Full normal defence; target share ~35% (estimate)

When to buy

2-0-0 farms R10-R35 when safe; Bank R30s; BRFs R40-R60; Central R50-R60; stop before R40 if shaky.
