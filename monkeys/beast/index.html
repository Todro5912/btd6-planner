Utility

  • Stuns Bloons: Tyrannosaurus Rex, Giganotosaurus

Energy damage: no Lead/Purple/Camo at base. Merging same-path beasts adds Beast Power; tier 3 is the most cost-efficient. Each tier 5 effectively costs four tier-4 Handlers plus the upgrade. Alchemists can't buff it. Stats page v54.0.
