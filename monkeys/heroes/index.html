Heroes

Towers Heroes Paragons

18 heroes. Tier is how beginner-friendly a hero is (S is best).

HeroPriceUnlockTierBest for
Quincy $540 Start B Free all-round early game on almost any map; good with Berserker Brew. v57 adds a Focus toggle at L4.
Gwendolin $725 Lv 14 B Lead/DDT support and fire towers (Dragon's Breath, Ring of Fire, Signal Flare); abilities help R63/R76/R78.
Striker Jones $700 Lv 21 C Bomb Shooter and Mortar builds; L8 pierce/range buff also helps Spike Factories.
Obyn Greenfoot $650 Lv 28 A Druid, Skywarden and other Magic builds; Jungle's Bounty income. Often called the best free hero.
Captain Churchill $2,000 Lv 15 C Early-mid carry on long straight maps; you must save up to place him. Slowest leveler.
Benjamin $1,200 Lv 15 A Standard/casual games and MM farming: cash and lives every round. No income in CHIMPS or Deflation.
Ezili $550 Lv 25 C Deep Freeplay (MOAB Hex % damage) and Necromancer builds; weak early. L9 strips Camo/Regrow/Fortified.
Pat Fusty $800 Lv 15 B Maps where he reaches every lane; Rallying Roar for many-projectile towers; T-Rex synergy.
Adora $1,000 Lv 25 A Super Monkey / Sun Temple builds and Boss events; slow leveler, weak in CHIMPS R50-R80.
Admiral Brickell $900 Lv 35 C Water maps with Sub/Buccaneer armies; must be placed on water. Reworked in v57 (weaker early, stronger late).
Etienne $650 Lv 25 B Replacing Radar Scanners with the L8 global-camo UAV; weak before L10.
Sauda $600 Lv 25 S Easiest strong early carry on Beginner/Intermediate maps; needs help mid-late game.
Psi $1,000 Lv 35 S Advanced/Expert maps, CHIMPS and obstacle-heavy maps; attacks anywhere through walls.
Geraldo $750 Lv 35 A Flexible shop items; Boss events; Paragon Power Totems (L20). Beginners tend to overspend.
Corvus $1,325 Lv 35 C Skilled CHIMPS/Half Cash play with spell micro; highest-skill hero.
Rosalia $875 Lv 25 A Maps with one active entrance per round; Ace/Heli builds (L6 discount); strong early-mid.
Silas $850 Lv 25 B Ice Monkey and Coldchain Skywarden builds; Flooded Valley, #Ouch. Hard early game.
Dan D'Monke $650 Lv 35 A Leak insurance, mid-game Reposition (free Chinook-style move), Macaque form for ABR.
