Lead Bloons

First on R28

Lead needs non-sharp damage. Darts can't.

Lead ignores Sharp, Cold and Energy attacks.

Good to know

  • Bombs, Mortars and Alchemists pop Lead from the start. Glue needs Corrosive Glue (2-0-0).
  • Alchemist 2-0-0 (Acidic Mixture Dip) lets the towers it dips pop Lead.
  • A Lead pops into two Blacks, so a Lead answer must also handle Black.
