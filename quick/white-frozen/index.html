White and Frozen Bloons

First on R22

White ignores ice. Frozen Bloons can't be popped by sharp attacks.

Every fix below pops both.

Good to know

  • Bloons are Frozen when your own Ice Monkey freezes them, so this mostly matters with Ice.
  • Ice Monkeys can't pop White until a path unlocks it.
  • Any Lead answer also pops Frozen for most towers.
