Camo Bloons

First on R24

Camo needs detection. Towers without it won't shoot them.

Spike Factory spikes on the track still hit camo. A plain Mortar doesn't: it needs Signal Flare (0-0-3).

Good to know

  • Camo is a property, not a colour: any Bloon can be camo.
  • One Village 0-2-0 (Radar Scanner) gives every tower in its range camo detection.
