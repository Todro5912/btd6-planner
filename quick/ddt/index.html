DDTs

First on R90

A DDT is camo + Lead + Black, and fast. One tower must handle all three.

Sharp attacks can't hurt them (Lead), and neither can explosions (Black).

Good to know

  • A DDT moves at about MOAB speed x 2.6, so it needs damage near the exit too.
  • Village 0-3-0 with Radar (0-3-0 includes 0-2-0) lets towers in range hit DDTs.
