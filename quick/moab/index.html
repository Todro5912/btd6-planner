MOAB-class Bloons

First on R40

Almost every attack hurts a MOAB. You need damage, not a special key.

The fixes below get extra damage or control against blimps.

Good to know

  • First MOAB: R40. BFBs from R60, ZOMGs from R80, the BAD on R100.
  • Save an ability for R40 if you are short on damage.
  • MOAB-class Bloons are immune to most slows and freezes unless an upgrade says otherwise.
